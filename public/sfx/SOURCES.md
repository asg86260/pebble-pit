# Where the yard's recorded takes came from

Every file here is cut from one of two packs by Kenney (kenney.nl), both released under **CC0** (public domain: free for any use, no credit required): **Impact Sounds** and **RPG Audio**, downloaded 2026-10-18. They were trimmed, moved in pitch, filtered and leveled for their lane (DESIGN.md, "The yard's sound set, by lanes"). The three magic sounds were rendered from Surge XT factory patches (GPL-3 synth; rendered audio is ours).
