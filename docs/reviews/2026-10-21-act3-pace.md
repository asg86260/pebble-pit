# Act 3 pace against the idle members' rebel rate (2026-10-21)

Measurement only; no game behavior changed and the knob below is not committed.

## What was run

Knob (temporary, uncommitted): `AFTER_AGITATE_REBEL` in `src/config/after.js` multiplied by env `IDLE_REBEL` (default 1).
That one constant is every idle member's line, both the ones on the podium spots (`agitating`) and the ones in the
crowd (`crowdLines`), so it is the whole idle rate. The pair's own lines (`AFTER_PODIUM_REBEL`) are untouched.

```
IDLE_REBEL=<1|0.5|0.33> node tools/node/act3-pace.mjs --carry <0|4|8> --seed <1|2|3>               # hands on: --hand 0.25 (default), --pop 0.85, --human 0.7
IDLE_REBEL=<1|0.5|0.33> node tools/node/act3-pace.mjs --carry 0 --seed <1|2|3> --hand 0             # hands off
```

`act3-pace.mjs` already had `--carry`. The hands-off rows are `playAct3({ hand: 0 })`, which is what
`test/after-hands-off.test.mjs` runs (that test differs only in turning the clock through `run` instead of `__fast`).
Strike = the `the strike` milestone; station columns = the `<station> stands` milestones (the first station bought is
always the mill); members = `union()` at the strike; rebel = `S.after.rebel` at the strike. The chain's other stations
(workshop, chipper, papermill, press) are in the per-run table; the "longest idle-buy" column is the longest stretch with
nothing bought. 36 runs, 4 at a time, 45 s wall each. Game minutes throughout.

## Which idle rate lands where

No idle rate puts the hands-off strike in the 52-63 band on every seed, because the strike is bimodal (about 58-60 min or about 75 min) and the idle rate is not what picks the mode: seed 3 strikes at 58-60 at all three rates, seed 2 at about 75 at all three, and seed 1 is fast only at 1.0. Idle 1.0 is the nearest: two of three seeds in the band, mean 64.6. Idle 0.5 and 0.33 are indistinguishable from each other (means 70.3 and 69.7, one seed in three in the band). So if the question is which rate keeps a hands-off player in 52-63, it is 1.0, narrowly, and by the seed rather than by the rate. For a hands-on player, carry 4 and carry 8 strike at 42-48 min at every rate (1.0: 41.9 and 44.0; 0.5: 45.9 and 44.4; 0.33: 43.3 and 47.6), so they sit 4-20 min under the band whatever the idle rate is, and no tested rate moves them toward it; the idle rate is worth about 1-4 min there, inside the seed spread (up to 10 min). Hands-on at carry 0 is 72-108 min at every rate. Nothing here supports half the agitator rate as a pace lever: it roughly halves the rebel banked and leaves the strike alone.

## Odd things

- Rebel is never what the act waits on: 50k-690k is banked at the strike in every run, and the strike is gated by support (signs and posters inking rims, `src/after/union.js`), not by rebel. Lower idle rates only shrink the unspent pile (hands-on carry 8: 243k, then 119k, then 90k).
- The slow hands-off mode is a stall near minute 60: the plan is fully bought and the longest nothing-bought stretch is 15-17 min, ended only by the strike itself, against 4-6 min in the fast mode. That 16-min gap is the whole difference between 58-60 and 75; I did not diagnose what the support spread waits on.
- Hands-on at carry 0 is slower than hands-off at carry 0 (90, 82, 82 min against 65, 70, 70): the papermill stands at about 21 min against about 14.5 and the press at 38-56 against about 32. The hand's work (default `--hand 0.25`) seems to starve the rosters rather than help them; not diagnosed.
- Carry level 0 still reports "the yard's carry 2 a load"; carry 4 and carry 8 strike within about 3 min of each other, so carry past 4 buys no pace here.
- n=3 is thin: the same config varies by up to 33 min across seeds (hands-on, carry 0, idle 1.0: 75, 86, 108), more than any idle-rate effect.
- The knob was a throwaway: the working tree for these runs carried it; this commit holds only this file.
