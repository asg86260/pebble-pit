The constrict's orb: a release timer

Today the orb has two ways out, and the bar shows only one of them. It lets go silently when the constrict's turn ends (MECHANICS[4].last = 12 s from the cast, no timer drawn). Or it breaks when its hit points run out (hp: 0.15 of the level-1 serpent's depth): every party blow lands on the orb first, and a click on it is a punch ORB_CLICK_X = 3 times over. Lifting the fighter out also breaks it. The 12-cell bar over its head is hit points, not time. So this is a mechanic change: time becomes what the bar shows, and a click takes seconds off it.

In each stage the orb closes and rises with the fighter, the time drains, two scripted clicks knock chunks off, and at zero it bursts and the fighter sinks home as it does today. Click a stage to knock off another chunk yourself.
