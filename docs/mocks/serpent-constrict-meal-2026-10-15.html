The serpent eats with its constrict

A mock drawn from plain shapes on the deep's cell grid, not the game. The meal is the constrict attack aimed at a creature: the comb lights head first, purple bolts jag from the crest tips, the orb closes round the nearest creature on the glass, then the orb and the bolts are reeled in to the jaws and swallowed, a lump runs down the body and the heal lands. The three variants differ only in the reel. All panels run the same deep from the same seed; a meal is due 12 s after the last one started, and "meal now" makes one due at once.

Taken from the game's own constrict (render/serpent.js drawOrb, orbRing, the crest; config/deep.js ORB_*; the orb row of SERPENT_PHASES):

  • The warning is the orb's 1.5 s: the comb lights head first, spike by spike. A lit spike's tip is white and its other cells flicker between the two top purples at 12 Hz; unlit spikes are the top purple but one.
  • Three bolts leave from the lit tips (tip index (n*5 + shape*3)). Each has 14 steps with sideways jag up to 4 cells, shrinking toward the target, every third step white, the rest bright purple. A shape is held a sixth of a second (floor(age*6)).
  • The orb is a ring of single cells, max(16, 2πr) of them, squashed to 0.9 tall. Every fourth cell is the bright purple and the rest the next rung down. It turns in held eighth-second steps of 0.075 rad. It closes from 1 to ORB_R = 5 cells over the first 0.2 s.
  • Variant C's lift is the fight's rise: held 2-cell steps, fast at first, over ORB_RISE_S = 1.4 s, with two fainter rings trailing it through the first half.

Not in the game, new for the meal: bolts held past their 0.5 s, for the whole reel, with the comb going dark tail-first so the bolts' roots retreat toward the head; the reel path; B's orb shrinking; the swallow of the orb.

Left out of the meal's orb: its hit-point strip and the clickable arrow, since nobody fights this orb. ORB_R = 5 cells is the fighter's orb; the larger creatures (the angler is 10 cells long) fill it edge to edge.

What the mock cannot show: the real coil's sway, lattice, ribs and wound; the deep's smoke, fighters and plinths; the camera and the deep's true 130-row height (this window is 200 by 110 cells, drawn one pixel a cell and scaled).
