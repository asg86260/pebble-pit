The serpent eats by constricting

Mock only, drawn from plain shapes on the deep's cell grid: not the game. Each panel runs the same deep from the same seed: the coil, its head at the left, three roaming creatures, and the serpent's health top-left draining between meals. A meal is due every 12 s from the last one; "meal now" makes one due at once. An eaten creature's slot stays empty a while, then the next kind swims in from an edge.

What the mock cannot show: the real coil's sway, lattice, ribs and wound; the deep's mote smoke, plinths and fighters; the camera and the deep's true 130-row height (this window is 200 by 110 cells, drawn one pixel a cell and scaled). Six of the roster's eight kinds are drawn from the game's own sprite grids (sardine school and siphon left out). The game's own constrict lights the comb head first and jags bolts from it before its orb closes; A and C borrow the lit comb and the bolts for their warning, then close a loop of coil instead of the orb. Faded things fade by opacity only. Timings are first guesses.
