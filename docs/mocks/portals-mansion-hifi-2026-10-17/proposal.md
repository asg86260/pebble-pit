**The portals and the mansion (proposed, 2026-10-17, from the owner's
"there should be portals to heaven and portals to hell" and "the game ends
with the squares getting a massive mansion to live in after freeing God").**
Each new place gets a standing portal, as the deep has the wizards' portal:
a way that is clicked and rippled through, with the pointing hand at both
ends until the first round trip is made, then faded by opacity. **Heaven's**
is the ghost road (beat 9) made into a way: it opens over the house's ruin,
where the souls rose from, and stays open after act 3 as the way between
the yard and the union's camp. It is the deep portal's negative -- a white
mouth in the white sky, rimmed in churning cloud, a dashed ink fringe
turning round it, purple motes drawn up into it -- either a tall tear with a
cloud stair down onto the rock on the ruin (H-A) or an upright lens on the
ground with a beam of light out of its top (H-B); its ripple rings purple
then pale. **Hell's** is burned open by Satan going ahead of them (beat 8):
white-hot with black veins flowing through it, a lip of rock lit from below,
embers rising in held steps, a few purple cells on the seam for Satan's
magic, and no red (red stays the sparks'); its ripple rings are jagged and
stepped and throw embers. Either it is a slit in the deep's floor, the way
beat 11 already goes (L-A), or a crater burned into the yard's ground
beside the ruin (L-B), which could instead be a shortcut opened after the
first descent. **The ending** replaces the old house with a mansion many
times its size: freed, God gives them their lives back (beat 15), they walk
home, and as they pass, the crew fall in behind them while the builders
raise the mansion floor by floor at the yard's left end -- a piece a trip
from a pile, bottom course first across the whole front, off a scaffold
that fades away once the last piece is set -- topping out as they reach the
gate. The crew and their lines gather in two files, the freed souls of the
afterlife come down and gather pale beside them, fireworks go up, and the
sheet. Three shapes are on the mock: A, a manor with towers, wings, a gate
and garden on the house's own ground, built from the broken rock that
killed them; B, a castle on a hill raised at the far left, with the ruin
and the old bench left below as a memorial; C, a long house with a window
for every line, wings that would grow with the lines. Calls for the owner:
H-A or H-B, L-A or L-B, which mansion, whether heaven's tear stays open in
the last scene, and what the end sheet says.
