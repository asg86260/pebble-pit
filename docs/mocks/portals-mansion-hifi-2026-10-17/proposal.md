**The portals and the mansion (proposed, 2026-10-17, from the owner's
"there should be portals to heaven and portals to hell" and "the game ends
with the squares getting a massive mansion to live in after freeing God").**

**A portal is the camera's, never a body's.** Each new place gets a standing
portal, as the deep has the wizards' portal: a way the player clicks and the
view ripples through, with the pointing hand at both ends until the first
round trip is made, then faded by opacity. That click is the only place the
ripple appears. No body ever goes through a portal: carriers, ghosts and the
freed souls walk the road, every step of it, so nothing teleports and the
carry keeps its length.

**Heaven's portal (H-A recommended).** A tear opens over the house's ruin
when the throne is empty and stays open after act 3, the way between the
yard and the union's camp. It is the wizards' portal's opposite: a white
mouth in the white sky, a soft cloud rim, a slow fringe whose every cell
eases on and off, and purple motes drawn up into it. The mouth is a light
source: on the dark page it is drawn landed, as hell's heat is, so it stays
light on both pages instead of going to soot. A stair of cloud lets down from
its lower lip to the yard floor beside the rock, in steps two cells tall with
an ink rim under each tread, and the ghosts walk it. H-B, an upright lens
with a beam of light, is kept for the vote. But it has no road of its own
(the carriers would still need H-A's stair), and it reads as a white flame,
which is hell's signifier. Cloud, stair, motes and the calm fringe are
heaven's alone.

**Hell's portal (L-A recommended).** Burned open by Satan going ahead of
them (beat 8). **Satan's route:** he sinks through the cloud floor, falls
through the yard's sky into the drowned pit, and sinks down through the abyss
and the deep's water. He is a body the camera can follow the whole way, and
he burns through the deep's floor where he lands. What he leaves is a wide
slit of white heat that throws its light up onto the deep above it. Black
veins flow through it, its lip is rock lit from below, embers rise and thin,
a few purple cells sit on the seam for Satan's magic, and there is no red.
Its hand only appears from the descent (beat 11). The carriers walk across
the deep's floor and climb down a cut ladder through the slit, and back up
it. L-B, a crater burned into the yard by the ruin, is kept for the vote as
**camera only**: the carriers never use it, so the carry's length stands. The
under-lit lip, the veins and the embers are hell's alone.

**The build.** The builders are the player's builders, however many there
are, and **the rock that killed them is the pile for every design**. The rock
is the one material that lies on the house's ground already, so nothing has
to be carried to a heap first and "built from the rock that killed them"
holds whichever mansion wins. What is left of the rock, a few cells, stays in
the garden.

The work goes in this order:

1. The scaffold, a level at a time. Each pole's six-row section is carried up
   and set, then each plank bay is carried and laid.
2. Every piece, carried from the rock to the nearest pole, up it, along the
   plank, and set. Bottom course first, so the mansion rises floor by floor.
3. At the top-out, the scaffold comes down the same way, a section and a bay
   a trip.

Every piece, section and bay fades in over about 0.3 s from its set, and out
as it is lifted.

**The honest sum** is the carries (pieces plus the scaffold up and down) times
each carry's own round trip at a body's walk, divided by the builders. It has
to be measured against the cage barrier's stretch, which has no number yet:
`BARRIER_BLOWS` is unwritten. The mock prints the sum with its assumptions: a
walk of 20 cells/s, a second to set, pieces of twelve cells, and a stretch
of 4, 12 or 30 minutes. With 8 builders, A is 387 carries and about 5
minutes.

**The ending.** Freed, God gives them their lives back (beat 15): each rim
piece fades from grey to black in turn, and the outfit darkens with them.
The walk home is the finale's: the crew fall in behind the pair in two files.
The ending is quieter than the finale:

- one volley of fireworks at the gate;
- the crew drift back to work, because everything keeps running;
- the freed souls walk down the stair from the still-open tear and stand on
  the ground.

The last held shot is the pair walking to the bench under the door and
sitting, then a window lighting, then the sheet. Every position is on a whole
column, and a three-cell body stands centered on a doorway of odd width. The
one exception is the old house's door, which is the game's four cells
(`FINALE_DOOR_W`), so a body stands in its left three.

Three mansions, three silhouettes, each with something of theirs in view at
game scale:

- **A, the manor of the rock:** towers, wings and a gate, with a lintel
  carved from the rock over the door, the rock's last chunk in the garden,
  and the old bench under the door, eight cells wide with a back.
- **B, the house, grown:** the old house at three times its size, with its
  door grown into the entrance and its bench by the door.
- **C, the hall of every line:** a massive block with a window for every line
  the yard has had. At twenty a floor, its floors are the line count over
  twenty, so it grows with the yard. The old house is rebuilt in front as its
  gatehouse.
