Combo Rhythms by Class

Each fighter's own rhythm, from how it fights: a hit on each tick, the big one tall and purple, grey where it resets and rests. Every rhythm keeps the class's damage a second as now. The slow classes -- Hexer, Sapper, Mage, Bard -- already breathe and are left as they are. A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game: a plain body for every class, a fist or a shot for its hit, no numbers.
