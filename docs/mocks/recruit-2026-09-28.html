The Recruit

Every station's first fighter: it stands on the new plinth beside the packed crate and fights at once, weakly, until rung 1 makes it a Fighter, Novice, Scout, Adept or Apprentice. Three ways it could attack, each looping, and a choice of how it reads as untrained.

A mock drawn from plain cells on the game's 6 px grid and grey ramp, not the game. It cannot show: the real serpent and its dents, the kits of the other classes beside it, the swim up from the pods, or the numbers.
