The serpent's explosive resist

When a Sapper is the highest-rung fighter, the serpent now resists explosive damage. The ward and the plates show magic and physical; explosive needs a look on the hide and a tile over the bar. Pick one look, on the hide or as a barrier under it, and a tile.
