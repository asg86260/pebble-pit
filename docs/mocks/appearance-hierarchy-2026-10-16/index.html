Appearance: the slots around the square

The owner, 2026-10-16: "another pass on the outfit menu, right now the hierarchy is a bit wonky, hard to read the tabs and what they correspond to" -- and then: "have the slots appear around the square, and clicking the slot opens the options below in the grid." Item names are never cut short.

This is a mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. Every frame below is live: click a slot, a tile, or the you / your sqouse switch. The purple brackets are the pad's focus; "play the pad path" walks it. Words marked like this are new placeholders for the owner; everything else is a message the game already has.

What the mock cannot show

  • The real drawings: the square, hats, backs and auras here are rough stand-ins (the expansion hats, every back and every aura are guesses), and nothing moves -- the auras, capes and balloon are still.
  • The game's font metrics: this page uses the system monospace; the measured name heights below are for this face and should be re-measured in the build.
  • The sheet's own scroll, the held-sheet chrome, the name keyboard (pad letter grid / Steam), and the 4-column branch's exact info-line height (assumed 2 lines, 36px).
  • Touch: the phone frames are drawn at 360 wide; real phone scroll and the one sheet seat are not reproduced.

Today

The page on main (9659d36a), scene wardrobe: two tab strips that look alike, the slot strip not reading as belonging to the square, counts as loud as names, the heading repeating the tab.

D1. A cross around the square

Rationale: each slot sits where it is worn -- head above, aura below, back and pattern on the back side, face and neck on the side the square faces -- with a leader to the body part, so the button is the label; the chosen slot is inverted and its same inverted tab heads the grid below, so the two read as one thing.

 

D2. Three on each side

Rationale: the same mapping in two columns, ordered top to bottom by height on the body (head, back, aura on the left; face, neck, pattern on the right), so the stage is one button row shorter and the grid gets that height; on a phone the six drop to two rows of three under the square, and only the chosen one draws its leader.

 

Both: what moves where

  • The slot tabs are gone; the six slot buttons around the square are the only slot control. On desk each shows the worn item's drawing on a grey body, the slot name in bold, and the worn item's name under it (full, wrapping). On phone, the drawing and the slot name only; the worn name is in the grid and the info line.
  • The heading "head: top hat" is gone. The grid's box carries an inverted tab with the slot name (the same inverting as the chosen button), and on its right, quiet in grey, the count ("9 of 24 open", today's message) and the slot's die.
  • Counts leave the slot buttons entirely: one count, beside the grid it counts.
  • You / your sqouse becomes the top of the page: a segmented pair with each square's mini portrait, framing everything under it.
  • Dice: name's die and title's die stay on their rows; the slot's die moves to the grid's tab row; randomize all stays in the foot.
  • Tiles show the name only, full, wrapping at spaces (and at a hyphen); what opens a locked item goes to the info line on hover / focus, as today's "gold crown: sqouse freed".
  • Info line fixed at two lines (the 4-column branch's), the foot unchanged: randomize all | clear, back.

The pad path (both)

  • LB / RB: you / your sqouse, from anywhere (as today).
  • D-pad on the slots: the nearest slot that way, spatially, as every page's nearest-that-way does; up from the top row goes to title, then name.
  • A on a slot: opens that slot's grid and puts the focus on the worn tile. A on a tile: wears it.
  • B in the grid: back to the slot button the grid belongs to. B on the slots: lets go of the sheet (today's B). This is the one change of meaning: B is two steps from a tile instead of one.
  • LT / RT: the previous / next slot from anywhere, in the order head, face, neck, back, pattern, aura (as today's slot tabs); in the grid it stays in the grid.
  • Y: rolls the slot the focus is in (a slot button or its grid), the name or the title (as today). A pad hint in the grid's tab row ("B slots") is optional and not drawn.
