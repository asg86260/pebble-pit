The crusher, second round: four quieter furnaces

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. Round one (the maw, the quern, the mortar, the gear drums) was too busy; these are calmer and furnace-leaning: a few big shapes, mostly white, grey and black, and the purple kept for the fire alone. Each is 30 x 30 cells, the crusher's own box (CRUSHER_W x CRUSHER_H), with the mouth open across the middle 20 columns at the top where the hopper counts a toss today. The left third is where the counter card sits over it in the game, so nothing that matters is drawn there: every door and flame is in the middle or right. Painted as the deep paints a station, a cell a character in its five tones as seen (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * purple), on a stand-in for the deep's floor. Left is the game's size (a cell is 6 px), then four times that, then the rest frame and the working frame side by side. White squares to its right toss scales in; while scales go in (S.crushAt recent) the second frame shows -- the fire climbs and brightens -- and sparks or a puff leave by the door or the flue. What it cannot show: the real abyss field, the scale bed, the serpent overhead, the pile-full mark and the counter card.
