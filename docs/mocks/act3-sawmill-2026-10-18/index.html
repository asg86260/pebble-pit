the saw mill, the frame saw, three ways

The owner: the frame saw overall is good; the frame and the saw need work, and a blade short of the frame's height is wonky. Each take below keeps the flywheel on its post, the rod and a press-style frame, and runs the blade the frame's full height, top beam to bottom, guided in it. The teeth are the approved handsaw's (saw 3): a three-deep sawtooth, the face on the slant, the back square, hooked down so it cuts on the down stroke. Drawn at the yard's scale: a cell six px at one times, a body three cells. The body holds the wheel's knob; the wheel is fourteen cells across. A small crank by the hub drives the rod, so a turn of the wheel is one stroke of the blade, three cells.

      • The gesture: the wheel here turns on the clock; the player's grab, spin and coast (after/gestures.js) are not wired.
      • The view from above: the blade cuts along the log, so a true side view would show it face on and the cut would not show. The planks past the blade, split by a white seam, are the drawing's way of saying "cut", as built.
