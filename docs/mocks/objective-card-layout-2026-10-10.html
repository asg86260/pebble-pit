Objective Card Layout

A mock from plain HTML and CSS, not the game. It cannot show the real glyph drawings (stand-in pixel shapes here), the fades between states, the queue card under it, or where the card sits against the notice card and the yard.

What is awkward today (shots of propsoffer, propsqueued, farmstep at 800 wide, where the cap is 250px; at 1280 the same rules let it reach 490px):
  • The line is one long unbroken run, so the card's content width is already past the cap and it stretches to the full max-width: max(120px, calc(50vw - 150px)) (style.css 1940). Everything below the line then spreads to that width.
  • The bill is justify-content: end (style.css 1984): "200 / 400" is flung to the right edge, a gulf of white between the glyph and name on the left and its numbers on the right.
  • The buy button is a grid item, so it stretches across the whole card with its one small price sitting dead center in a wide empty bar. Queued, the cancel is pushed right with justify-content: flex-end (style.css 2000), under a name flung left.
  • The cap follows the window, not the words: huge on a wide window, and 120px on a 390px phone, where nothing fits.

Every option takes its width from its content up to one cap written in characters (34ch and so on), so the card hugs short copy and wraps long copy. Copy is the game's own (objectives.js, rows); the amounts are the real bills with made-up "have" numbers.
