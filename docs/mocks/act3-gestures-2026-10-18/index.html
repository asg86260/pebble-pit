Act 3 hand work (mock)

Every station is worked by its own motion. The faster you do it, the faster the work is done, up to a cap: a gauge on each panel fills with how fast you are going. A grey ghost hand shows the motion until you have done it three times, then fades out. Every panel is live: mouse or touch. This page is drawn from plain cells on the 6-pixel grid, not by the game. Every word on it is a placeholder (marked like this).

Recommendation, pending the owner's yes: only two crits stay, and both are moments of timing: the pedestal's big, fast bubble (pays 5×) and the stump's yank as the roots give (out in one pull). Every other station has no crit; going faster is the reward.

What the mock cannot show

  • The real renderer: these are flat cells drawn by this page, not after.js, its layers or its camera.
  • The real camera scale: the panels are three times the yard and the thumbnails one time, as close as a page can get.
  • Gamepad feel: the pad lines are text only. Nothing here reads a pad.
  • Pacing and numbers: every speed, band, window and payout is a guess for the pace tool to move.
  • The stations in the camp: each panel stands alone, with no hauling, no posts and no members walking in.
  • Sound.
