MOCKThe arch's answer, on a phone

A phone stood upright (390 px across) at the yard's zoom shows 78 cells of the yard. The arch over rock 15 is 116 cells wide, so today both feet are off the frame for the whole scene. Four ways out, each drawn on the same ten-second loop, plus today's phone and a desk window for comparison.

Measured in the game (rock 15, own dev server, 390×844 and 1280×800): arch x 5190, w 696 (116 cells), view 468 world units (78 cells) at zoom 0.833; the camera held at camX 5304, the span's middle, with 19 cells of arch off each side. At 1280 wide the view is 1536 units and the whole arch fits. Widths by rock: the arch passes the phone's 78 cells from rock 6 (80 cells) and tops out at 120 cells from rock 20; props and net top out at 104, the dome at 120. The zoom ladder: at a 1x phone, 5 px a cell is the yard; 4 px shows 97 cells (fits the arch up to about rock 10); 3 px shows 130 (fits every shield with room). On a 3x phone a step is a fifteenth of a cell, so a glide from 5 to 3 would be six visible snaps, which is why no option here glides the zoom.

What this mock cannot show

  • It is drawn from plain shapes on a 6-unit cell, not the game: the rock, the arch, the crew and the clouds are stand-ins, and the receded yard is a flat gray-out, not render/recede.js.
  • The real camera glides in from wherever the player was looking (CUT_SETTLE_S); every panel here starts already on the span.
  • The real answer (the hold, the set-down, the pair's beat under it) is shortened to one held impact; the timings are first guesses, not config.
  • It runs at 1 px a CSS pixel. A real phone at 2x or 3x renders the same cells crisper, and the size of a cell in hand (how small 3 px a cell feels) only shows on a phone.
  • It does not show the boards, the counter or the corner buttons, which a smaller zoom (C) would leave the same size over a smaller yard.
