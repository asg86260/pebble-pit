Controls page: three candidates

This is a mock from plain HTML, not the game. Fonts, sizes, capitals and spacing are copied from the real controls page, but nothing here is clickable and no yard is behind it. A dashed outline marks what is new. Grey words in [brackets] are placeholders: I have not written any player text, the words are yours.

1. The 1 – 9 row

The number keys on the keyboard jump the camera to a place: 1 goes to the rock, 2 to 9 go to the buildings, right to left (src/input.js:743, 795-812). This is the controls page with one extra row for them.

2. Phone rows

What the controls page would show when the touch setting is on, in place of the mouse rows. Phones cannot focus or pick up workers at all: those are the right mouse button only (src/input.js:141), so those two rows are simply gone. Each pair stacks, input over action, as the real page does on a narrow screen.

controls
tapinteract drag on dust[sweep] drag elsewheremove camera two fingers while sweepingmove camera

What each row is in the code

  • tap: a tap at a building opens or shuts its board, a tap on the rock hits it (input.js:314-324, 192).
  • drag on dust: a finger that lands on dust sweeps for its whole length (input.js:210, 643-673).
  • drag elsewhere: a finger off the dust scrolls the yard, with the phone's own momentum (input.js:63-72, 208-211).
  • two fingers while sweeping: a second finger turns the pair into a camera pan (input.js:158-162). With no sweep going, two fingers also scroll.
  • no keyboard row: a phone has no 1 – 9 or arrows, so those rows are gone too.
