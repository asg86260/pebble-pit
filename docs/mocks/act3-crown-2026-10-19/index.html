Act 3: God's throne and crown, nine combinations

A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. God is a redraw of the built figure, face (c) serene, with his breath and the crown slipping and set straight by his left hand (held poses, the built timings). The glare, halo and rays are the built ones, simplified. The stair under every throne is take 1, the cloud switchback, as voted. Stone and cloud use four tones only: paper, pale grey, mid grey and ink. God, his crown, glare, halo and rays are raw light on both pages, so they stay the brightest things. It cannot show the camera's pan or dive, parallax, the crowd thinning, the gaze, the roar, the unmasked face or the signs.
