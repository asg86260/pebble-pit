Serpent Bar Overhead

The bar bigger and moved down from the top of the glass to just over the serpent, the portal moved up to make room. Every blow's number is knocked off the hide as now, then flies up into the bar, and only when it lands does the bar drop: a grey trail where it was, draining after a beat. The heal's number swells and flies in the same way, growing purple back onto the end. A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game: no camera, one stretch of coil, three fighters' worth of blows, the four defense pips as squares.
