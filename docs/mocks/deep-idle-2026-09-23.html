The deep's crew at rest: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The left end of the deep's floor with the crusher, the pods, the altar and the circle as plain blocks, and six idle hands on it: three gatherers waiting by the crusher on a bare floor, one fresh out of a pod, two scribes whose circles are full. Each body keeps to its own station's standing ground (the game's DEEP_STAND_W, a body's leash here). Bodies are drawn as the deep draws them, the yard's white square turned over. It can't show the serpent, the weapons at work, the real window's size, the ripple, or how any of this reads with forty bodies down there. Call one to work sends a resting body on each panel to the altar, straight from wherever it is (mid-float, mid-hop), and lets it go again a few seconds later.
