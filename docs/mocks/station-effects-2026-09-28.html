Station Choice Effects

Every choice on a station's board shows everything it leads to. On the pick (a built, unchosen plinth), each base unit lists the two specialists it forks into and each one's move (rung 4) and capstone (rung 8). At the fork, each specialist's row lists its move and capstone. Three layouts, all with the game's own wording.

A mock in plain HTML styled like the board, not the game's board. It cannot show: the pip tree over the fork's rows, the bills' coin marks, a phone's narrower board, or the fade when a branch closes.
