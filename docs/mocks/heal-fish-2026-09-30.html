Serpent Fish Meal

The heal is banked instead of trickling: fish drift through the deep on a clock, the serpent eats one with its mouth, and the whole bank lands at once, the same total a second as today. A meal is a lunge, a swallow that runs down the neck to the belly, and the heal there. The bar at the top gains the lump in one step. Each pane brings the fish a different way. A mock drawn from plain shapes on the cell grid, not the game: the head is on the left, the bar has no pips, and the numbers are made up.
