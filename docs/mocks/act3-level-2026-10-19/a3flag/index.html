MOCKUnion flag logos page

Drawn from plain shapes on the game's 6 px grid, at a near plane's size and at a far plane's size (the small row). It is not the game. Six emblems for the union's flag, each in paper on the union's ink, the flag rippling in held steps: 1 the raised fist, redrawn; 2 the pair -- the two squares side by side, the union's own founders; 3 the crown, toppled upside down; 4 a broken chain; 5 the crowd -- nine squares in a block; 6 two squares joined, hand in hand.
