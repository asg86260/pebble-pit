Serpent recoil

Mocks for DESIGN.md, "Endless serpent": past the story each emptied health bar is a level. The serpent reels for a beat in a held pose, then the bar fills for the next level and the number on it ticks up. Three poses; vote one. A is built as the guess, and the vote is one line (RECOIL_POSE in src/config/deepcrew.js).

This is a mock, drawn from plain shapes on a canvas, not the game. The coil is laid from the game's own numbers (length, the head, neck, girth and tail taper, the belly, the sway, the crest, the jaws' hinge and gape, the cell grid) and the recoil's timing is the built one: a smear frame of 0.08 s past the pose, the pose held 0.7 s, back in three held steps over 0.36 s, the bar refilling over 0.6 s.
  • What it cannot show: the fighters, their blows and numbers, the creatures and the meals, the orb (it bursts on the recoil's first frame), the statuses and the stun ring, the hide's ward and plates, the fang dropping from the belly, the real bar's shards and its pixel-font label (the number here is the page's font), and the camera; the body is laid along x rather than along its arc, so a steep bend is a little thinner here than in the game.
  • The smear: the built recoil's smear frame is the pose overshot (1.35 times as far) for one held frame. The "ghost" switch adds the line it left behind, a dark trail of the rest pose for that frame; that is not built, and is a second vote.
  • The rest line (an aid for this page, not in the game): through the recoil, where the body would be without it, in dark grey behind, so the three shapes can be told apart at a glance. Pause to hold a frame.
  • No shake, no white flash, no squash tween: every frame of the recoil is a held pose on the cell grid.

A. Rears back (built)

The head is thrown up and back off the line, the jaws wide, the neck following; the belly and tail stay where the blow caught them.

Reads as
A cry: the head is the part of him the player watches, and it is where the jaws already open for a meal and the roar.
Cost
The head rises toward the bar; at seven cells it comes up to it.

B. Buckles at the wound

The body folds down where the bar was emptied, at the belly, as if the blow went in there; the jaws half open.

Reads as
Hurt, at the place the cracks run from. The head barely moves.
Cost
The fold dips toward the stations under the middle of the coil.

C. Arches

The back arches up in the middle, head and tail held low, the jaws half open.

Reads as
A flinch of the whole body; the biggest shape of the three.
Cost
The roar's bump runs along the same stretch; this one holds still where the roar travels.
