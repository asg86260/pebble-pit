The janitors' roomba: three options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The closet, the bodies and the poop are stand-ins on a 6-pixel-style cell grid; the bodies wander at random and squat far more often than the game's ten minutes, so there is something to clean. It can't show the real routes (heaps, the pit's mouth, the sites a body is held off), the real paces, soot off a stack, the sky's muck arriving with a front, or the real closet sprite and cap stand. The counters under each panel are poop taken in, and how long the average patch lay before it went.
