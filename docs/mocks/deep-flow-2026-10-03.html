Deep Creature Flow

A steady stream of the eight creatures crosses the deep, both ways, at three depths: far, middle and near. Every one enters from off the glass and leaves off the other side; none appears in the water. Now and then the serpent takes a near one, which turns from its path to the mouth and is eaten. No trails behind any creature. Each pane draws depth a different way. A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game: the serpent is a straight band with simplified jaws; the meals come every 6 to 12 seconds as in the game.
