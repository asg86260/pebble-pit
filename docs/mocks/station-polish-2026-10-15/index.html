Station polish: the eleven props, two directions

The deep's eleven props (src/deep/props.js PROPS, as on main at f4928882) next to two polished redraws. Each prop is drawn on its plinth, before rung 8 and at rung 8, on black water, in the deep palette only: six greys, five purples. White is left for the kits and bodies.

  • A, cleaner and iconic. The same silhouettes, tidied up. Each part gets three tones: a lit edge on the top and left, the body, and a shadow edge on the right and bottom. The thing a class trains with (bag, bell, target, pot) is a step lighter than its frame. Each prop has one small purple class accent, and nothing else is drawn on it.
  • B, richer and more detailed. A's silhouettes with a fourth tone: a deep corner, a cast shadow under an overhang, and a wider shadow side on round things. B also adds material detail (wood grain, nails, bolts, staves, straw texture, stitching, a ladle, staff lines, a folded page corner) and a little character (the dummy's painted target and stitched face, quillons on the swords, a ribbon with two tails).

The light: both options are lit from the upper left with the grey rungs. Today's purple light along every top edge is gone, so purple stays the class accent. Before rung 8 the accent is the one small mid-purple mark each class carries: pommels, clapper, ribbon, bullseye, dagger pommel, brew, spark, bookmark, note head. At rung 8 that mark turns bright, and the parts that already turn purple today (tape, guards, bell, staves, rings, belt and dagger, handles, hoops, text, note) turn purple with their shading kept.

What this mock cannot show: the fighter standing at the post (the dotted 3x3 square marks where the body stands, five cells right of the prop and seven for the Swordsman); the kits in their hands; the scale heaps beside a station; the kit marks, rung pips and the works stacked over a station; the build scaffold; the motes and weather drifting past; and the light page (the deep is drawn inverted there). Colors here are the abyss ramps' raw tones, not the dark page's mapped ones. Only the hexer's bubble moves, on the game's own beat (BREW).
