Station round three: the crate and the lectern

The owner: the default station (the crate) is a bit wonky, and the lectern still reads badly. Each card shows today's prop as it is in the game, then four directions. All of them keep A's register: three tones lit from the upper left (A's shading rule, unchanged), one small purple class accent, the rung-8 parts turning purple from rung 8, and roughly today's footprint.

What is wonky about the crate today: the brace does not run corner to corner (it stops two cells short of the bottom-left and clots into a three-wide block at the top right, trapping one lone board cell), the planks are uneven (2, 2 and 3 rows), and the straw sits left of center with the lid's bare right end poking out, so the box looks skewed.

What this mock cannot show: this page is drawn from plain cells, not the game. It cannot show the fighter at the post (the dotted 3x3 square marks where the body stands), the kits, the scale heaps, the kit marks and pips, the scaffold, the motes or the light page. Colors are the abyss ramps' raw tones, not the dark page's mapped ones.
