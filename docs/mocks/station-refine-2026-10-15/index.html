Station refine: the lectern and the dummy

Option A of the station polish was voted in. The owner asked for the lectern (Mage) and the dummy (Assassin) to be refined. Each card shows A as voted, then three refined directions. All of them use A's register: three tones lit from the upper left (A's shading rule, unchanged), one small purple class accent, and the rung-8 parts turning purple from rung 8. Each prop keeps roughly A's footprint. The Bard's music stand is also shaped like a lectern, so it has a card too, in case "lectern" meant that one.
