Wardrobe back slots (mock)

The scarf's treatment (the voted B, "low and long") carried to the back slots: marks in 2 px units rather than whole 6 px cells, held poses that stream behind on the walk and hang when standing, proportions fitted to the 18 px body. Drawn from plain shapes on a bare body, not the game; the "today" column is copied from the real draws in src/render/wardrobe.js (the gold-trim cape from the wardrobe-expansion branch). Each panel loops: walk right, stand, walk left, stand. Poses step at the game's own clocks: the walk at 4 a second (WARD_STEP_HZ), the balloon's bob at 1.2 (WARD_BOB_HZ), a flag at the yard flags' FLAG_WAVE (8 frames over 6 s). Big canvas 4x, small one 1x.

In the deep

The same, drawn in the yard's inks and turned over, as the deep turns its picture over (render/deep.js); the gold is drawn so the turned picture shows the deep's own gold. At 2x.

What this mock cannot show

  • The yard: no crowd, no camera zoom, no station hats, no head items or scarf worn with these (a scarf's tail and a cape share the back; that pairing is a call for the build).
  • A carter: its cart is its back, so none of these are drawn on one.
  • The real walk: here the body slides at a steady 12 px a second and the "walking" flag flips the moment it stops; the game reads walking off motion with a 0.25 s hold (WARD_STILL_S), and bodies walk at their own speeds.
  • A crouched, seated or knocked-flat body, a load over the head (which the balloon and the flag would have to clear), the picker's tiles, and device-pixel snapping at odd zooms.
  • The deep panel's turn is a CSS invert of black, white and one gold; the deep's own greys are not in play because none of these options uses a grey.
  • The moneybag (wardrobe-expansion) is not shown; it already bounces a unit on the step.
