The furnace's fire: three idle loops, each with its own per-scale particles

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The furnace is CRUSHER_SPRITES.rest copied verbatim from src/deep/sprites.js, in the deep's tones as seen (# white, + and - the grey ramp, o the black water, * the top of the purple ramp), on a stand-in for the deep's water and floor. Only the eight-by-twelve inside of the door arch is redrawn each frame -- no frame is ever swapped -- plus the few cells each scale gives off. Three gatherers to the right toss loads of three scales (three frames apart, forty frames in the air, as GATHER_TOSS_*) on their own rhythms, about two scales a second in all. Each scale that lands warms the fire a little (it swells for about a quarter second and eases back over a second); every particle fades down its own tone ramp, never an alpha and never a flash. What it cannot show: the real abyss field, the scale bed, the counter card over the furnace's left third, the serpent overhead, and the real gatherers' walk to and from the scale bed. Add ?t=2.3 to the address to hold one moment.
