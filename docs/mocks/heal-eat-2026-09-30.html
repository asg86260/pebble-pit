Serpent Jaws

Three ways for the serpent to take a creature, each with its own heal. In every pane the jaws open all the way and the creature goes in between them, cut by the jaw lines rather than a straight edge. The lump then travels down the neck in 2.4 s instead of 0.85, and the heal shows on the whole body when it lands. The meal still fits the shortest wait (6 s): a 3 s swim, a 0.8 s gulp, a 2.4 s swallow. Each loop brings a different creature: skull fish, blob, eye. A mock drawn from plain shapes on the cell grid, not the game: the body is straightened out, and the game's head is rounder.
