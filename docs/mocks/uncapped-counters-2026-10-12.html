Uncapped counters

A mock from plain shapes, not the game: it cannot show the real walk (pips filling by thirds, the leaving clock), the hats and glyphs as the game draws them, the hover lines, or the counts moving at real yard speed. Brim glyphs are stand-ins: a notch in the middle for a cart, a notch at each end for a forklift.

Reading of the ask: "miners" is the boulder's diggers (the quarry's miners already have seat pips). The diggers keep the - and +; the house's carriers have none, since haulers are the remainder. Typical counts in the game: diggers up to a handful (the gang is 5, the breaker hat capped at 3); haulers 100+; carts and forklifts single digits. Every pip is one body, black, no empty seats, since neither counter has a cap to be empty against.
