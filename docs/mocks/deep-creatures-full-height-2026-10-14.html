The deep's creatures, the whole height (fifth pass)

A mock drawn from plain shapes plus the game's real sprite grids (FAUNA_SPRITES) and the deep's palette, at one cell per canvas pixel, scaled up. It is not the game. It cannot show: a click's reaction or a fight's scare, the fight itself (the coil is a plain band), the zones' dim in the midnight band, the water's stars, or the dark theme's mapping (every tone here is as the light theme shows it).

Each frame is a window 232 cells wide and the deep's full 130 rows tall, the floor under it. Creatures leave one end and come back in at the other, 30 cells past the edge, as today; they move a whole cell at a time and stay at least 8 rows above the floor.
