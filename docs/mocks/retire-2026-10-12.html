4 · Gameplay questions

  • Does the station keep its class and rung, inherited by the new fighter? Probably yes: the gear is the station's, not hers.
  • Does the retired pair still count in the crew? Probably they leave the count, and her pod (if she had one) frees up.
  • What is "retire" after the kill, given the serpent reforms (deep-kill-reform)? Is the fight over for good, or does a new phase or a new serpent come? The replacement fighter only matters if there is still something to fight.
  • Who is the replacement? A gatherer or delver pulled off the deep's own work (nearest, but leaves that job short), a spare from the yard down the shaft (keeps the deep whole, costs the yard a body), or nobody until the player assigns one.
  • With nobody spare: does the station stand empty with its gear waiting until a body is free (a new pod, a hire), and does it get the pile-full style warning mark while it waits?
  • Finale: does the yard's work pause for the parade (the crowd is every worker), and does the deep's crew join it or stay below?
  • Finale: does the celebrating crowd stay at full ink, against the cutscene rule that everything the scene is not about recedes? The mock keeps them full.
  • Finale: the house they go into, which one is it: the house that is already the sqwife's, or a new one that appears? (Nothing may pop in.)
