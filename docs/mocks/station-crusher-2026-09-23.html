The crusher, redrawn: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The crusher is the deep's purse: gatherers (haulers lent down the shaft) scoop the loose scales off the floor and toss them in an arc into its mouth, and a scale is money once it is crushed in there. Each drawing is 30 x 30 cells, the crusher's own box (CRUSHER_W x CRUSHER_H), with its mouth open across the middle 20 columns at the top where the hopper is today, so the tosses still land where the game counts them. It is painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as they are seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), on the deep's mottled floor. The crusher has no dome in the game today, so none is drawn here. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that. White squares to its right toss scales in; while scales are going in, the working part moves (two frames) and grit puffs off the foot, as the rollers turn today only while it is fed. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water here is a cheap stand-in), the scale bed on the floor, the serpent overhead, the pile-full mark, and the counter card that sits over the crusher at the left of the glass. The crusher is drawn by code today (drawCrusher in render/deep.js), not from SPRITES: building one of these means painting its entry at crusherRect() in place of that code, plus its second frame for the moving part.
