Feedback form

Mocks for DESIGN.md, "Feedback form (design, not built)". Three shapes of the same form -- a box, a tick that sends the save, a send -- each on the held sheet (play.html) and in the landing page's column (index.html), in five states. Vote one; then it gets built.

This is a mock, drawn from plain HTML, not the game. The sheets reuse the game's own rules, copied from src/style.css and src/title.css; the tick, the line of what goes, the fold and the kind buttons are new and are the proposal.
  • What it cannot show: the yard behind the sheets and the held sheet's wash, the phone's bottom-sheet seat and its drag, the pad marker walking the rows, a real send (sending and failing are faked; the "network" switch below picks which happens), the sheet's fades, and the size of the real open slot (the figures are the deepest fixture, 57 KB gzipped).
  • The words: anything in grey italics in brackets is an [owner's words] placeholder: every word a player would read that the game does not already say. Words already in locales/en.ftl (settings, back, controls, copy save, stopped) are shown as they are. The values on the line of what goes (version, language, platform, save size) are data, not copy. The text typed into the box in the filled state is a made-up player's.
  • Clickable: type in a box, tick the tick, press send; in C, pick a kind. The state buttons set every frame at once.

In every option the line of what goes sits between the tick and send, so it is read on the way to the button, and it is built from the same object the request carries: the save appears on it only while the tick is on. Failed keeps the words and the tick; nothing is queued to send later.

A. A page of its own, off settings

One button on settings under controls opens a pane with the box, the tick, the line and send, and a back. The way controls already works; the settings page grows by one row.

Every stop is a row the d-pad already knows: the box (A types, on the desk the Steam keyboard), the tick (A toggles), send, back.
Phone

One pane (data-pane="feedback") on each page. The least new shape: everything on it is the sheet's.

B. Compact: it folds out on the settings page

No page of its own. The button on settings inverts and the form unfolds under it in a frame, as load a save's paste box and the cloud's section do; send and a "never mind" stand as a pair. Pressing the button again folds it away.

C. It leads with the kind of report

The page opens on three choices; picking one shows the form under a line naming it, with a way back to the choice. Picking "the words" puts the language on the kind line as well, so a reader sees which file they are reporting on. The kind goes with the report.
