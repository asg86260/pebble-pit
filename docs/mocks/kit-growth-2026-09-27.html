The owner, 2026-09-27: "Do animations/kits change after upgrades? We should review that first." Today a class's kit never changes across its eight rungs; only the keystone (rung 4) changes the attack, and the capstones (rung 8) have no visuals. This page draws the chosen answer for the three starting classes, all nine of their branches: a small mark of the branch from the keystone on, and at rung 8 a touch for capstone A or B that grows on that mark, with a small change to the attack.

Costs: across all ten classes, 30 branch marks plus 60 capstone touches and 60 small attack changes, 150 pieces, each one to three cells, and every attack pose has to carry its branch's pieces. Buys: the two choices a player makes on a station, the branch and the capstone, each show on the fighter, and the rungs that are only numbers add nothing.
