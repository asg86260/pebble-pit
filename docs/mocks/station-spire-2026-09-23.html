The spire, redrawn to its work: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The spire is where the wizards stand and channel: each holds a purple beam on the serpent's coil for as long as it is in reach, the board's two ladders are beam strength and curse the heal, and the called star is pulled down onto the coil from here. Each drawing is painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as they are seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), under a dome sized from the drawing's inked width (4 cells of pad, 4 of wall), on the deep's mottled floor. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that. Two wizard squares stand at the foot and feed a thread of purple into the spire, and the spire throws the beam up out of the dome toward the coil (a grey stand-in band at the top); the bodies have a keyline the game's do not. What it cannot show: the real abyss field, the scale bed, the serpent and the star, the board and fence. In the game the beam leaves from the wizard's own body, not from the spire's head, and a station is painted once into an image, so the threads, a beam from the tip, and anything that glows or turns would be new drawing code, not a sprite edit; the sprite alone is only the still picture.
