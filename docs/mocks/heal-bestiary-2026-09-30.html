Serpent Bestiary

The fish that come to the serpent's mouth, one a meal, from the left edge of the deep. A species changes how a meal looks and feels: its size (and so how far the swallow bulges), its path, its colors and what it sheds. Never what it heals: every meal lands the bank, the same total a second as today. Eight of them, each on its own loop, all eaten at the same beat. A mock drawn from plain shapes on the cell grid, not the game: the the creatures are drawn in code cell by cell, and the head is a wedge with hinged jaws.
