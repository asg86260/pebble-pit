The portal, in the world: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. Every panel is painted by a copy of the pit's own abyss field (the same flow, breathing stars and veil, the same tone ramps), so each portal is made of the liquid rather than laid on top of it. It can't show the wizard pouring it, the deep's end (which would get the same treatment, turned over), the ripple, or the real window's size. The field's turn is its real 52 seconds, so the veils drift slowly; the portals move faster.
