First muck: a cutscene, three ways

This is a mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. It cannot show the real camera, the real cloud and rain drawing, the real crew sprites, timing at the real pace (a front brews for 40 s; it is squeezed here), or how the yard around the shot keeps moving. Colors are the game's own: ink, rock greys, cloud and storm greys, the grey water, and the brown of the dust and the muck (MUCK_TONE, SMOG_TINTS.dust).

What actually happens, in order (all three tell this, no invented steps):
  1. A rockhand's swing puts up a faint brown dust puff (later, the machines' stacks put up soot).
  2. It climbs and settles into the sky; the clouds darken by what is up there.
  3. A front brews (the clouds swell), then rains: grey water, and among it brown drops, one per marked mote.
  4. A brown drop that lands lays a cell of muck on whatever it hit.
  5. On the rock, the cut or the plots, muck eats the swings first; on open ground a hauler walks over and shovels it.
  • Captions are placeholders in brackets. The words are the owner's.
  • In the game the yard never pauses for a cutscene and any click (or a held space) skips it; the mock loops.
  • Cells fade out over held frames here where the game takes a shoveled cell away in one go.
