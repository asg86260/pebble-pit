Reading the fight: four ways to show the serpent's damage and heal

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. All four panes run the same scripted twenty-two seconds of fight on a stretch of coil, so they can be compared frame by frame: a brawler punches (6 a blow), a lance sticks and bleeds (3 a half second), a grenade bursts and its rings cross three lengths (25 each), a scribe's sigil holds the belly, a wizard's beam burns (8 a half second) and curses the heal, a star lands (60), and then nothing strikes and it heals shut. The wound and its cracks, the gap and the cage are drawn in all four, as the game draws them; what differs is only what each option adds. The coil is the game's plain white hide on the deep's black water, a cell is 6 px, and each pane is shown at twice the game's size. What it cannot show: the real abyss field and camera, the real bodies and weapons (squares and lines here), a fight with many weapons at once, the four stages' tones (this is the bare coil throughout), and the numbers are the mock's, not the game's.
