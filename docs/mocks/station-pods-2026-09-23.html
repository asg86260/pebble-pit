The pods, redrawn as the deep's homes: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The pods are where the deep's crew lives: each pod bought is one more of the crew, and its body walks out of it onto the floor. So each option is ONE pod's drawing, 8 x 6 cells (the game's pod is 7 x 5 today), stacked the way the game stacks them -- four abreast, a cell apart, bottom course first -- seven pods here. Painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), on the deep's mottled floor. The pods stand bare in the game, with no dome, so none is drawn here. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that. Every few seconds a body (a keylined square, not the game's sprite) steps out of the newest pod and walks off, which is what buying one does. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water here is a cheap stand-in), the scale bed, the serpent overhead, the board, the builders putting a pod up, and the game's real bodies. The pods are drawn by code today (drawPods in render/deep.js), not from SPRITES, so the copied entry is a per-pod drawing the renderer would stamp once a pod.
