Drawn from plain shapes at your window size (2000x967), on the game's 6 px grid. It is not the game. It leaves out the camp, the bubbles, the gaze and the burning.

Kept from the last round: everything is squares, every shape one flat tone, and the crowd is the packed mass. God is the only white and the only outlined thing, and he stands behind the crowd. New here: the throne stands up out of the crowd so you can see it, and a line of souls climbs to him. The line's souls are ink on the light page and a light grey on the dark page, so they read against the sky, the steps and the throne.
