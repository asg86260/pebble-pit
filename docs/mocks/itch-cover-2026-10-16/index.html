Itch cover, with the title

Four covers for itch.io, 630 x 500, each beside the 315 x 250 thumbnail itch shows in its browse grid (downscaled smooth, as itch does), on a light and a dark page. Every picture is a real shot of the game (its scenes, HUD and roster hidden, the press's scanlines kept, its vignette off); the only thing added is the title, in the store art's own 5 x 7 letters from tools/steam-art/capsule-draw.js, each letter pixel a whole number of cells, and loose motes cleared from under it. No tagline: title only.

What it cannot show: how itch crops a cover on a narrow phone grid, and the title is not yet a drawing in src (the game's own title page sets its name in type).
