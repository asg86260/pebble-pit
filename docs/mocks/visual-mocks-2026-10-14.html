Visual audit mocks

Three items from the visual consistency audit (docs/audit/visual-2026-10-14/) that the owner asked to see before anything is built: C14 (one number face), C21 (material masses in one tone) and C20 (the deep's shades). Each section draws today first, then the options. Vote per section.

This is a mock, drawn on plain canvases, not the game. The cells are copied from the code: NUM_FONT (config/deepdraw.js), the counter card's measures (render/counter.js), the roster's boxes (roster.js), SHADES/depthShade (grid.js), MUCK_TONE/MUCK_SKIN, the star's reds (FIND_COLOR), CAULDRON and the hut's BOARD grain (render/apothecary.js), ABYSS_TONES/ABYSS_MAGIC_TONES, PLINTH_TONES, DOME_TONES, PROPS/MAT/R8_PARTS/BREW (deep/props.js) and the place.js layout of a station.
  • What it cannot show: the real yard around these pieces (the scenes are hand-set, the heap and the hill are drawn shapes, not the sand sim), the world zoom changing (the yard here is at zoom 1, the C21 and C20 strips at 2 screen px a game px), the deep's flowing water, veil and fauna, the fighters' kits and poses (each fighter is the plain square), the serpent, the hats (one stand-in hat), and the game's press scanlines over the frame.
  • Dark page: the yard canvases are mapped by lightness the way ink.js does it; the deep is drawn as it is seen on either page (its tones turned and turned back). The theme button cycles system / light / dark.
  • Magnify draws every canvas at three times, nearest-neighbor, to see the pixels.

C14. One number face on the canvas

Today the counts over the yard (a stand's hats, the garage's forklifts), the roster's numbers and the corner counter are fillText in the page font at 13 px: anti-aliased and off the grid. The deep's fight numbers are the pixel NUM_FONT at 2 screen px a font pixel (the dark chip at the right of each frame is a blow's number, as the fight draws it, for comparison). The same scene, four ways.

NUM_FONT as it stands

Every glyph, at 4 px a font pixel. A digit is 4 wide and 6 tall, one font pixel between glyphs.
Missing glyphs
- (a negative fmt, which no count shows today), t (fmt says 1t past 999.96b dust), and , (the decimal comma sayNum already flags for localization). Nothing these counts draw today is missing: digits, +, ., k, m, b are all there.
Today
13 px ui-monospace, drawn centered over its point. The counter's digits are a different height from its 9 px marks and blur at every zoom.
A. NUM_FONT, 1x
One screen px a font pixel: 6 px tall. On the grid and crisp, but half the height of today's numbers; on a phone it is about two device pixels a stroke.
B. NUM_FONT, 2x
The fight's own size (NUM_FONT_PX = 2): 12 px, about today's height. One face for every number on the canvas, yard and deep.
C. NUM_FONT, 2x, keyline
B with one font pixel of paper round each glyph, the way the deep's choose-mark is keyed, so a count reads where it lands on a heap or a dark roof. The fight's numbers keep their grey drop line; the yard's take the keyline.

C21. Large one-tone masses that are material

Each row is one mass: today, then dealt from neighboring rungs by a hash of the cell (the PLINTH_TONES pattern, so it never strobes the way shadeNear's dice would), at two strengths: mild leans on the mass's own tone, strong deals its neighbors as often as itself. The notes under the strips say which rungs each deals.

Muck band
Today one MUCK_TONE body under a MUCK_SKIN lid. Mild: one cell in three takes #68503a, the roomba's middle tone, already in config. Strong: a step either side (, mixed from MUCK_TONE), and the lid deals two.
A hill's deep core
Today depthShade bands, and everything thicker than the fifth band is one flat #111. Mild: one cell in four deals the next rung lighter (so the core is #111 with #2c2c2c in it). Strong: one in two deals a rung either side, clamped. Depth still reads; the bands lose their hard edges.
The star's crust
Today the deepest red, flat, round a flickering core of the two bright reds. Mild: one crust cell in four deals the third red. Strong: half. The crust never takes the core's two reds, so the core stays plainly hotter.
The cauldrons
Today flat #000 sprites beside the hut, whose wall is grained in BOARD (#000-#151515, which hardly shows: compare the wall at the left). Mild: the pots take the hut's own BOARD. Strong: #000, #111 and #2c2c2c (ink and the two darkest SHADES) for pots and wall alike, so the grain can be seen.

C20. The deep's shades

The owner: keep the abyss more mysterious and visually different, but clean up the shades. What is actually there: the deep already draws from one grey ramp (ABYSS_TONES, twelve faintly blue rungs) and one purple ramp (ABYSS_MAGIC_TONES). The spread is in how it is dealt: twenty-one material runs in MAT, plus PLINTH_TONES, DOME_TONES, the floor and the pods each picking their own rungs, nine rungs of grey in use (2 through 10) on one strip, every cell a different pick. Off the ramp: one mote tone (#9b9ba6, the fleck), and deepdraw.js re-types ramp values as hex literals instead of indices. The finale's house (yard side) brings a fifth grey family, FINALE_GREYS.

The strip below: three stations on the deep's floor (the brawler at rung 8, so its purple parts show, the monk, and the hexer with its brew bubbling), each a dome, a plinth, a prop and its fighter, with silt drifting in the water.

Today
Every material its own short run, picked anywhere from rung 2 to rung 10. Rich, but nothing says which rung means what, and a new prop picks its own.
A. Four bands, grain kept
One ramp, four named bands of two neighboring rungs each: shade 2-3 (dome inside, floor), dark 4-5 (leather, iron, rings, the dome's rim), stone 6-7 (wood, chain, the plinth's body), lit 8-9 (straw, steel, paper, bell, the plinth's top). White (11) is only for what is made and held: kits, the fighters' rule. Every material is one of the four; a new prop picks a band, not rungs. Close to today's look, with fewer greys and a rule.
B. Flat things, grained ground, purple light
Grain only on material: the floor and the plinth's stone. Props and domes are flat silhouettes, each one rung (props stone 7, domes shade 2), and their top edges catch a purple light from above (purple 8, breathing a rung up and down in held steps). Purple is "outside the normal": the deep is lit by it. The rung-8 parts stay bright purple.
C. The yard's grammar, inverted
The yard draws its buildings as flat ink on white paper; this draws the deep's props as flat water-black cut-outs against a lit dome. The dome is grained (it is the glow: shade 4-5) with a purple rim; the plinth is grained stone; props are one tone, the black of the water, so they read as holes in the light. Most mysterious; detail inside a prop (the bell in its frame) reads by silhouette alone.
