Steam Achievement Icons

Every achievement in src/catalog.js, all 49, drawn as Steam's 64×64 icon in three styles, each unlocked and locked, at 1× and 2×. Names and notes are the catalog's, as given.

This is a mock. The icons are drawn on this page from the game's own glyph maps (GLYPHS and BADGES, copied from src/glyphs.js), with the shelf's conventions imitated: a one-pixel stroke in the coin's color, the unbuilt "ghost" outline for a thing not yet had. It cannot show Steam's own treatment (the rounded corners and the darkening it applies in some clients), how the icons look in the overlay pop-up, or new drawings for the achievements that have no glyph: those wear the closest existing glyph and are flagged.

To decide

  • Hidden achievements. Steam shows every name and description on the store page unless an achievement is marked hidden. These give the surface away: . Suggest hidden; the owner's call.
  • Locked style. Steam needs a separate locked image. Drawn here the game's way (a thing not yet built is its outline in ghost grey); the alternative is the unlocked icon desaturated and dimmed, which Steam users expect but which reads as a grey smudge at 64 px.
  • Coin color. The stroke round the glyph takes the coin the achievement is about (blue ore, green crops, red sparks), as on the shelf. Or all black.
  • Tiers. The ladders (10/25/50/100 rocks, the pebble and crew counts, ore) are told apart by pips in styles 2 and 3, and only by the glyph's badge in style 1. Is a pip row enough, or does each tier want its own drawing?
  • New drawings. The table above lists what has no glyph; the drawing is the owner's (or a glyph-editor session).
  • No new words are needed: Steam takes the catalog's name and note as the achievement's title and description.
