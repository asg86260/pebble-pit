Seeing the crew

No question mark on hover any more. Hold the right button and the buildings fade back and the workers stand out; a worker under the cursor slows down instead of stopping. Each panel loops: three seconds as the yard is, then the right button held.

Drawn from plain shapes, not the game: the buildings are blocks, the workers are squares walking a flat ground, and there is no rock, pit, sky or board. A right press on a worker still picks it up, as now.
