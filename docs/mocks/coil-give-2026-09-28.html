Coil Give

How the serpent's body answers a blow, five ways. In every pane: a Brawler under the coil punching, every third a haymaker; a Ranger's arrows, each landing the moment it reaches the hide; a Mage's beam held on the coil, a stream that only ever presses. The fighters' knock-back is on in all of them. A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game: one stretch of coil, no bar, no numbers.
