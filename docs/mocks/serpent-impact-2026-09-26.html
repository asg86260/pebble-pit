Making a blow land: four ways the serpent could answer a hit

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The same scripted twenty seconds in every pane: brawler punches (small, often), a grenade's three bursts (medium), a called star (one huge blow), then punches again. Each blow is marked by a small white tick where it lands. In every pane the reaction scales with the damage, so a star reads as a star and a punch as a punch. No flash (cut before), no splats (the numbers stay as built). A, B and C are only the picture; D is also a rule: a heavy blow stuns the heal. What it cannot show: the real coil, girth, cracks and ribs, the numbers, the real weapons, or many weapons at once.
