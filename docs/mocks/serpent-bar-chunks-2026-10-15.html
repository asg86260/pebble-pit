Serpent Bar Chunks

Larger pieces off the serpent's bar. "Today" is the bar as built (400 by 14 screen pixels, its tones, its 3-pixel shards, the knock and the heal's purple). A, B and C break the same blows off in bigger pieces. Every pane runs the same stream of blows: small ones, a big one now and then, and a meal's heal flowing back in.

This is a mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. The numbers flying into the bar, the coil, the pips and the status row are left out, and blows are larger than the game's (a real blow is often under a pixel of the bar) so the loop goes somewhere. "Zoom" shows the bar's end at 3x so the pieces can be read.
