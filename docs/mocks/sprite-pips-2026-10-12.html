Sprite Over Pips

The chosen layout, at every station: a sprite on top says what is counted, round pips two cells across under it are the count, and the specialists are a counter of their own beside the plain hands, the way the house has carters beside workers. The hatted counter's pips are its hats: filled where a hatted body is at work, a grey ring where a hat waits on its stand. The plain counter's are its seats: filled where a body is at work, a grey ring where a seat is empty. No divider. Every pip block wraps at 6 and stops at 3 rows, the rest written "+N" under it. A station with one kind shows one counter, centered. The open question is the −/+: one pair framing both counters (i), or a pair on each (ii). Each strip is at 1x (one world pixel a screen pixel, a cell 6px; the game's own cell is 5px) and magnified 2x.

A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. The sprites are the game's own drawing code (drawBody, drawCart, drawCartBox, drawLiftBox from src/render/crew.js) and the hat rows from HATS / HATS_TIGHT in src/sprites.js, copied in and drawn here. The circles are pixel masks on whole world pixels, not arcs; at the game's 5/6 zoom they would be resampled, which this page does not show. It cannot show live counts (a pip filling in thirds as a body walks over, emptying as one leaves, the count easing), hover or the cursor over a pip or button, a dead (dotted) button, what a press does, or the station buildings as they really are; the farm, the rock, the quarry and the tower are stand-in shapes.

The parts

The sprites that head each counter, and the two pips, magnified 3x. A trade wears its hat (the counter's tight brim for the farm, as the roster draws it today).
