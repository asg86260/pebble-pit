Sprite Over Pips

Every counter in the yard becomes the same shape: one sprite on top that says what is counted, and round pips under it that are the count. A filled pip is a body at work; a grey ring is a seat with nobody in it. The −/+ stay where they are today, on the post's line at either end of the pips, a button and a cell clear, and the house's counter and the garage's keep today's 24px gap. Every case is drawn in both pip styles: circles two cells across, and one-cell dots. Each strip is shown at 1x (one world pixel a screen pixel, a cell 6px; the game's own cell is 5px) and magnified 2x.

A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. The sprites are the game's own drawing code (drawBody, drawCart, drawCartBox, drawLiftBox from src/render/crew.js) and the hat rows from HATS / HATS_TIGHT in src/sprites.js, copied in and drawn here. The circles are pixel masks on whole world pixels, not arcs; at the game's 5/6 zoom they would be resampled, which this page does not show. It cannot show live counts (a pip filling in thirds as a body walks over, emptying as one leaves, the count easing), hover or the cursor over a pip or button, a dead (dotted) button, or the station buildings as they really are; the farm and the rock are stand-in shapes.

The parts

The sprites that head each counter, and the pips, magnified 3x. Carters and forklifts are the whole body; a trade wears its hat (the counter's tight brim for the farm, as the roster draws it today).
