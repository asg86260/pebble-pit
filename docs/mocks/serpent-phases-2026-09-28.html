Serpent Phase Mechanics

One mechanic per phase, each answered by a class, so which fighters you picked matters. Phase 1 has none: it teaches the wound against the heal. Tick a panel's box to see its answer land.

In each panel the white bar at the top is the wound, the purple + numbers are the heal, and the white numbers are hits. Squares on plinths are fighters. Melee fighters (solid) fight at the hide, and ranged ones (outlined) shoot from their plinth.

A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. Time runs about four times fast, one cycle every ten seconds; in the game each mechanic comes round every 30 to 45 seconds. It cannot show the real coil's drawing, the kits, sound, the stun's held sway on every body, or how the numbers feel over an hour.
