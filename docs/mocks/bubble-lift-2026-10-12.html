Bubble lift

The constrict's bubble today rises three cells and sits where the station's board opens. Three ways to lift it clear, each played through: the bubble forming, the rise, the hold with its bar, clicks landing, the release, and the fighter back at its post.

This is a mock. It is drawn from plain cells on a 6px grid, not the game. The bubble, its bar, its arrow, the bolts and the burst copy today's sizes and timings (ORB_R 5, bar 12 cells, 3 cells over the bubble, burst 0.6s, the turn 12s). The deep, the coil and the station are stand-ins. The dashed box is the board's footprint at about a 1080p window; the real board is sized in screen pixels, so its height in cells changes with the window.
