The index: three shapes

A mock from plain shapes, not the game. The glyphs are the real drawings from GLYPHS (src/glyphs.js); the yard is a stand-in. Names are the strings the game already shows; every entry's text is a placeholder for the owner's words. It cannot show: the real held sheet's fade and size, the real yard under a look mode, the live numbers moving, hover timing, or dark mode on the real canvas. Muck, poop, pollution and the pit have no glyph yet (dashed boxes) -- a new drawing each, through glyphs.html.
