A health bar for the serpent: four shapes

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The same scripted twenty seconds in every pane: blows open the wound (stage one), a pause lets it heal back, a second flurry breaks the bare coil, then the warded stage opens partway and heals again. The bar reads the serpent's health as what is left of the stage: full at a closed wound, empty at the break -- so it drains under blows and refills at the heal. Four stages, four defenses, so every option shows where you are among the four as well. What it cannot show: the real coil, weapons, numbers, camera and the deep's inverted tones; the fading stage dimming the bar; how it sits beside the counter card.
