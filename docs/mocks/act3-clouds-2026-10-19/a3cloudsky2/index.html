MOCK, drawn from plain shapes at your window size on the game's 6 px grid. It is not the game. The floor, the crowd and the throne are G1, unchanged. The sky is S3's cloud ranges, now made by math alone: each range's ridge is seeded noise over position and slow time, its billow heads swell, shrink away and come back by noise, and its white top, shaded body and line all follow from the shape, so the sky never repeats, is the same for the same seed, and changes as you watch.
