MOCK, drawn from plain shapes at your window size on the game's 6 px grid. It is not the game. The floor, the crowd and the throne are the last round's G1, unchanged; only the sky above the horizon is new. Every take builds its sky from the floor's billow: white tops, a shaded underside and a line along the top, kept a step quieter than the floor (no ink by day) so the floor stays in front and the horizon reads. By night the clouds are not the day's inverted: they are dim masses whose tops catch the light and whose undersides sink into the dark.

What it cannot show: the game's real crowd, camera and other layers, or what tracing the floor every frame would cost there.
