MOCK, drawn from plain shapes at your window size on the game's 6 px grid. It is not the game. The game's camera: it only slides sideways, and depth is parallax -- everything slides at its own depth's speed. The cloud floor is G1's fixed perspective view, its rows sliding at their depth's speed (the mounds at your feet fast, the rows by the horizon barely); the cloud ranges slide slower still. On it: the workshops (plain blocks), the union's camp (the pair on the soapbox, their crowd), God's camp (throne, his crowd with the G1 lip, the planted flags), and the carriers walking between. The union's crowd now has its near souls as a pale body in a thin ink outline, small far ones solid ink, so it no longer outweighs God.
