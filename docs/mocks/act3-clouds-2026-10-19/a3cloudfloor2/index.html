MOCK, drawn from plain shapes at your window size on the game's 6 px grid. It is not the game. The floor is now one surface: a field of cloud billows laid out on the ground plane and seen through a real camera standing a little above it, so each billow is big and round at your feet and thins to a one-cell scallop at the horizon, and the rows bunch together with distance on their own. The whole field slides slowly toward you; the far billows creep, the near ones roll, and new ones grow out of the horizon rather than appearing. The souls and the throne's base cast a small shade on it. The throne and the crowd are the last mock's.

What it cannot show: the game's real crowd, camera and other layers, or what the floor costs a frame (here it is traced cell by cell every frame).
