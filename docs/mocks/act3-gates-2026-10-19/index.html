Act 3: heaven's portal as a stair up to pearly gates, three takes

A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. It replaces the purple tear over the ruin and the purple ring over heaven's cloud floor. Each take is drawn at the game's framing: zoom 1, 6 px cells, 3-cell bodies, at 2000 by 967 and 800 by 600, with 3x crops of the 800 frame. Stone, cloud and metal use the act's four tones only: paper, pale, mid and ink. The gates' light is the one exception. It stays raw white on both pages, like God's, so on the dark page it is the brightest thing on screen.

Each loop runs: idle with the hand, a click, the gates open in four held steps, the ripple out of the gates to heaven, heaven's side of the same gates, a click, the ripple back, then the gates close in four held steps. The "road" frame shows the walks with the gates open: souls rise out of the ruin and climb, and the ghost pair come down. Every word on this page is a placeholder.

      Recommendation

        What this mock cannot show

        • The camera. In the game the view glides and lands on heaven's own framing; here each place has a still camera, and the ripple simply opens from the gates' middle.
        • Where the two ends sit in the world. Today the yard end hangs 34 cells over the house and heaven's end hangs 14 cells over the cloud floor. Here the yard gates stand on the stair's top landing 30 cells up, and heaven's gates stand on the cloud floor with the light coming up through it. That moves heaven's end down onto the floor (ACT_HEAVEN_HANG near 0). The light between them is today's straight ride, now drawn as a stair of light.
        • The portal opening for the first time (as the souls rise at the house's fall). The stair would be let down a step at a time, faded in by opacity, and the gates would fade in after it. That is not drawn here.
        • Real bodies, wardrobe, the ghosts' looks and the real rock; the sky crowd and the camp are simplified redraws; the hand is the built glyph, inked in the act's tones instead of purple.
