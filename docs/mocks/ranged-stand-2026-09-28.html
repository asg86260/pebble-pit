Where a ranged fighter stands at its station

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. Each station is a grey dome with a white stand-in drawing inside and its glyph at the top; each fighter is a 3x3 white square with its weapon beside it -- the Ranger's bow, the Hexer's wand, the Mage's staff with its orb, the Sapper's charge on its fuse. Four stations side by side at the game's spacing (the gap between two domes is about seven cells), and the Ranger's again at twice the size. It cannot show the real station drawings, the fighters' kits or attacks, the heaps of silt that drift between the domes, or the walk up to a new spot -- which, by the house rule, every one of these would need (nothing teleports).
