Act 3 polish: the grove (pine) and the saw mill

The owner voted the pine and the wheel and bench, then asked for a polish pass: "they dont read super well", and "make each station a bit more exaggerated and just focus on the thing it does". This page is those two only; the chipper with the paper mill, and the workshop with the press, are polished on their own pages. The rest of the first mock is on index.html.

A mock, drawn from plain cells on the game's grid (P = 6, the game's greys, a body an 18-pixel square with a 2-pixel rim), not the game. Each station is shown before (as voted) and after (polished), at 1x and in a 2x crop, idle and working. The rules of the pass: one solid black mass with one signature shape pushed past realistic (the pine's stepped tiers; the saw mill's wheel), no detail under two cells except a clean line, grey only for material, and the working state carried by the body and the one moving part. At the foot, both polished stations stand beside two real yard stations cut from a 1x shot of the game.

Every word on this page is a placeholder. Names are working names (marked like this); the canvases draw only numbers.

Dark is ink #e0e0e0 on paper #1f1f1f, each grey mapped by its lightness the way ink.js maps it. It is not an inversion.
