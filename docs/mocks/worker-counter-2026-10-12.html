Worker counter, second pass

Mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. It cannot show the real yard sprites, the real body glyph, the yard's zoom, the screen-pixel digits, or real seat counts; stations are stand-ins and the walking bodies are boxes.

Each panel: quarry 3 of 5 with 2 hats, farm 7 of 7 (full), filter 1 of 4 with one walking over. The loop presses + on the quarry (a body walks over and takes the seat), + on the full farm (dead, nothing moves), and - on the quarry (the body walks off). Under the line: the house's carriers at 52 of 60 with 8 carts, for scale. Grey on the seats is the bill-line grey; the only color is none (no coin marks are counted here).

What the mock cannot show
  • How each reads at the yard's real zoom and over a busy ground (dust, rain, the quarry mouth).
  • Seat counts here are invented: the game would read them from capOf / roomAt, which today the roster never shows.
  • Deep stations (one fighter each), the forklift line, the wizard's hat-is-the-badge case.
  • Touch: hit targets are drawn, not tested.
  • The 0.6x strip shrinks the digits with everything else; the game draws its counts in screen pixels, so on a phone they would stay full size while the glyphs, pips and buttons shrink.
