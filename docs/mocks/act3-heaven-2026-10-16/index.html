Act 3: heaven, the look

Plain cells at the game's sizes (P = 6, a body 18 with a 2-pixel rim), not the game. Each scene at game scale and a 3x crop. Design: DESIGN.md, "Act 3: heaven (design, not built)".

1. The greeting (every loop)

The pair's souls come down onto the cloud, dressed as chosen (defaults: top hat, monocle, bowtie; bow, eyes, scarf), fading in by opacity in held steps. The crowd of the dead stands in files by line, rims in the pale grey, ticks over the head; the near files hop.

A. The cloud is the rock

High key: a great cloud hit like the rock, mist falling as pale grains onto a cloud floor; woken crew have black rims, the waiting dead pale ones.

B. The roll call (recommended)

A soul is rebuilt a rim piece a click (twelve pieces round the square), then walks off a body. The field goes from grey to ink as the act is played.

C. The climb

Heaven is tall: shelves of cloud with thin black edges, a stair to each, the gate a purple outline at the top, light falling from it.
