Serpent Bar Knock

The blow's number is knocked off the hide, held, and goes up into the underside of the bar. The bar should recoil as it goes in, away from it, and the health should answer. Each pane is the same two blows, one small and one big, with a different answer in the fill. A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game: the coil is a block, the bar has no pips or statuses, and the bar's knock amounts are the game's (4, and 8 for a big blow); the health resets each loop.
