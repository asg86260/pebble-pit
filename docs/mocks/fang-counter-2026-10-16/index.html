Fang count above the furnace

Three ways to show the fangs in hand on the furnace, in place of the counter card's fang row (now gone). This page is a mock drawn from plain cells in the deep's palette, the furnace copied cell for cell from its sprite. It is not the game. The big canvas loops: fangs fall into the hopper one at a time (the count goes up as each is crushed), then two stations are bought (the count goes down).

Now, for reference

The crusher scene (node tools/look.mjs crusher), cropped and blown up. Brick #42424a, mortar #26262c, the lit top edges purple #412465, the fire's purples and white.

Most fangs anyone can hold is six: seven slots, the first station free.

A Mark and number over the hopper

  • The build button's fang mark (9 x 9 screen pixels, as on the button) and the count in white, floating over the hopper's mouth.
  • At 0 the whole label fades out; the first fang fades it in.
  • A new count cross-fades over the old one; the mark glows purple and fades back.

B A plaque on the furnace's face

  • A small dark plate set into the brick over the door, on the cell grid: the fang mark, then x N in cell digits.
  • The plate stays once a fang has been seen; at 0 its fang and number dim to the mortar's grey.
  • A new count cross-fades; the plate's frame catches the light for a moment.

C One fang a fang, standing on the rim

  • The fang as it lies in the water, stood root down, one for each held, along the hopper's rim from the left.
  • At 0 the rim is bare. A new one fades in where it stands; a spent one fades out from the right.
  • No number to read: six is the most, and six fit, the first standing a cell over the rim's corner.

What this mock cannot show

  • The game's camera and zoom: here a cell is 9 pixels, three screen pixels at 3x; in the game a cell is 3 to 4 screen pixels and moves with the deep's framing, so how big A and B read changes with the window.
  • The fire's heat field, the sparks, the water's motes and the breathing light on the top edges: the furnace here is still, the fire a fixed picture.
  • The gatherer who carries the fang to the hopper: here the fang just drops in.
  • The counter card and the build button beside it, and the dark/light page tones (this is the dark page's deep only).
  • The digits are a 3 x 5 cell font made for the mock; the game would use its own number font or a cell font of the same size.
