Focus bracket idle - five directions plus none

Mock of the purple corner brackets standing still on a focus. Drawn from plain shapes on a 3x canvas (one cell = 18 px, the P = 6 grid at 3x). Every state is a whole-cell step held for the stated time; no tween, no fade of the bracket, no brightness change. The one-cell halo round the brackets is drawn as haloCells does.

What this cannot show: the real target art (the actual row card, real station sprites, the live deep water and its drift), the bracket's glide between stops (another agent's work; the idle here just loops from its rest pose, and should restart from rest on arrival), other zoom levels of the menu pixel grid, and how an idle reads after ten seconds of looking rather than three. The deep is black water in both themes, so its light and dark panels differ only in the page around them being the same: both use the bright accent rung.
