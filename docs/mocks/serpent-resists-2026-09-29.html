Serpent Resists

Each class deals one of three kinds of damage. The warded phase resists magic, the split resists physical, and the fading phase switches between the two every ~20 s. A resisted hit does half, as a class's weak phase does now.

The question here is how the hide shows which resist is up. Magic keeps the warded phase's purple ward as it is in the game. Physical is new, and there are two looks for it: A, plates in bands across the hide, and B, the scales standing up off its top edge. In the fading phase the next look flickers in for a second before the switch.
