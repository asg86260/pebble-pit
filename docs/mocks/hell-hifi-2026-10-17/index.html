Act 4: hell, high fidelity (round 3)

A mock drawn from plain cells at the game's sizes (P = 6, a body 18 with a 2-pixel rim), not the game. Design: DESIGN.md, "Act 4: hell, the mine and the forge (design, not built)". Hell is dark on both pages; the page around it follows the theme. Every frame is at 1x (game scale) or 3x, pixelated.

Placeholders. Nothing in a picture has words. Every player-facing word on the board (section 4) is a placeholder, tinted like this; the toggle below hides the tint, so the sheet can be seen as it would read. Ore, bar, tool and barrier names in captions are working names from DESIGN.md, in italics.

The palette. The deep's ramp, capped at six: black, the deep's ground #26262c, its stone #42424a, lit #6e6e78, pale #b4b4c0, and white. #121216 is gone. Rock is texture and made things are shape. Rock holds to two adjacent dark rungs, the deep's ground and stone, with lit flecks only near fires. Everything built (furnace, anvil, tank, trough, tray, stair, cage) is drawn a band brighter, with a one-cell black gap round its silhouette and a white bottom row where the floor's fire catches it. Heat with no red: light comes from below (the floor's top row is lit, the air just over it is washed, and a body's bottom rim is white); flames are white, their height and tone driven by smooth value noise over time, as render/flicker.js drives the cauldron's fire; glare is crossfaded between rungs by alpha; embers rise and fade out. There is no row-shifting shimmer, only a few faint motes drifting up through empty air. Nothing structural runs full height in a station tone: the flue is capped three cells over the furnace, like the yard's chimneys, and ladders and the cage's chain are drawn in the rock's own dark. Every ore face is set into the rock by a one-cell black gap, as shape() sets a station, and the motifs sit a rung under the stations, so a face never outshines a furnace. Purple shows in three places only: the road's portal, God's halo, and God's light going into the souls.

Which ramp hell should use. The scaffold's render/hell.js paints its placeholder floor from the neutral GREY ramp (HELL_FLOOR_TONES in config/hell.js). Proposed: hell draws from the deep's capped ramp through render/deeptones.js (GREYS, and PURPLES for the three magic places), with roles of its own beside DEEP_GREY: ground 4 and stone 6 for rock, lit 8, pale 10, white. The road from the deep then runs into hell as one material, the dark page already gets the deep's handling (landed, with the hollow written raw #000), and hell inherits the six-grey cap and its browser check. This mock draws in those tones as seen. On the dark page every tone goes through ink.js's mapping: written landed, then turned by the page's map, with the hollow written raw #000. In practice each tone is kept and held between the paper and the ink, so white lands on #e0e0e0. The rock's ground #26262c sits only just above the page's #1f1f1f (about 1.1 to 1), so the rock separates from the page by its stone cells and the floor's lit row, and each picture also gets a frame. Changing the theme reloads the page, as the game reinks by a reload.

1. The run of chambers: five camera stops at 1x, a phone, and a diagram

Each frame is the game's own view, about 133 by 100 cells (800 by 600 at its zoom; S.viewW = S.W / S.zoom in world.js), at 1x: one chamber, with the barrier it is working toward at its right edge. Stop 1, the landing: the portal in the roof and the muster's ladder, the anvil and smith, the quench tank, a furnace, the dross face, and the pair at rubble they cannot yet cut, its gaps leaking the cage room's glow. Stops 2 to 5: the stair comes in at the upper left, a hauler climbs it with bars for the anvil a step a column, a miner works the face under it, the furnace shows as many white tongues as its heat, a brimstone vent crusts, and at the right the wall gang (option (a), section 2) swings at the barrier while two haulers relay the spoil to the heap. Stop 5, the cage room: God in the cage on its chain, the glow filling the room.

The run, as a diagram

2. The stations, at 3x

The ore faces: one still motif an ore, readable at one cell

Each motif reads at one cell and holds still: dross mottled blobs, cinder iron horizontal bands, pitch copper pale one-cell diagonal veins on black, obsidian glass big dark facets with a white corner cell, adamant a lattice. A slow sheen crossfades over copper, obsidian and adamant as decoration only. Working: a ghost miner swings in held poses (rest, wind-up held, smear, impact held, follow-through), dust puffs off the face, every third blow a lump drops, and a hauler carries it off. The face is one cell deep where it has been worked. Each ore also appears at 1x beside its 3x.

The furnace: heat as white tongues, and a refused ore

A chamber's heat N shows as N white tongues in the door's bottom row. Cold: door black. Lit, heat 2: two tongues, with the stoker standing by. Stoked, heat 2: the same two tongues, taller and whiter, with the stoker throwing brimstone. Out of fuel: the bin is empty, the tongues are gone, the last embers pulse in the door, and the flue smokes. Refused: a heat-2 furnace with an obsidian lump (tier 4) waiting in the trough beside it. The trough is the furnace's strip, and the pile-full mark is warning() from render/pilemarks.js copied exactly (WARN_R = P * 2.6, bars of r / 4), hung where pileMarkAt would hang it: at the middle of the strip, seven cells under the ground.

Brimstone: the crust round a vent

The vent puffs and licks white (smooth noise). Crust grows round it a crystal at a time, each fading in. A gatherer knocks it off a crystal a blow and carries a lump away.

The anvil and the smith

Idle: the anvil empty, bars and the old pick on the tray. Working: a hauler brings the bars in, the smith works the blank in held strokes, 24 of them (TOOL_STROKES in config/hell.js), and white sparks fade as they fall. The blank cools by crossfade between heats, and the finished pick is carried out.

The quench: black water from the abyss

For the top two tools. A ghost brings a bucket down from the deep and pours it in. The smith dips the white-hot glass-edged pick, steam rises and fades, and the pick comes out dark.

The wall gang: a vote

The spec's one standing decision is the gang's size: "break through early with a big gang and the bars dry up." That only works if the blows a minute grow with the gang, so the gang's shape decides it. Two shapes are shown at the bone wall. Spoil goes to two haulers in both: a relay, one from the face to a set-down spot and one from there to the heap, so neither ever passes the other.

  • (a) Shoulder to shoulder, no walkway. All N members stand at the face, each swinging on its own clock out of step with the others, so blows land N times as often and the wall falls N times as fast. The members overlap the way a crowd at a station does, a few pixels apart. No new building.
  • (b) Rotation off a walkway. One member swings at a time. The next comes down a ladder at the walkway's end, waits on its low rung while the last walks out underneath, and steps in. The last walks back along the floor, up a ladder at the walkway's start, and along the deck (seven cells up) to the tail of the queue. Haulers carry spoil at their side, under the deck. The rate is one swinger, less the handover, whatever the gang's size: a bigger gang only rests longer. The walkway is also new infrastructure the spec does not have.

Recommended: (a). It is the only one of the two where the gang's size is the decision the spec wants: a gang of 2 against a gang of 4 is half the speed, and every member on the wall is one off a face.

The barriers, each worked by a gang of four, option (a)

A gang of four swings together at the face, out of step, stepping forward as the face recedes. A piece comes loose every three blows, falls as its own cells, and lies at the face foot. The first hauler lifts it overhead and sets it down at the relay. The second carries it to the heap, where it goes on cell by cell, every cell of it, each fading in, so the heap ends as big as the wall was. The wall is undercut, two columns at a time from the face, bottom to top. Each barrier reuses its seam's motif cell for cell: rubble is dross blobs with gaps that show the glow; the bone wall is cinder's bands as courses of bone, with knuckle cells; the lava gate is copper's diagonal veins as cracks, with fire showing white through them; once it is breached, fire runs out along the floor's top row and cools by crossfade. The anchor chains are obsidian facets as links, three strands staggered. The cage is adamant's lattice as bars, with God behind it. A 4-second hold and a fade end each loop; the fade is the mock repeating, not a game event. A strip of stills at 1x sits under each.

A new tool arrives, and the pair's own click

A miner at the bone wall with the dross pick: every blow rings off (white rings that open and fade) and nothing comes loose. A carrier walks in with the iron pick, the picks change hands, and the next swing bites and a piece falls. Then the miner steps back, one of the pair steps up, and two clicks (the pointer) land two blows of their own.

3. Assets sheet

Each at 3x. Lumps are 2 by 2 cells and bars 3 by 2, each in its ore's motif, so they read at 1x. Picks are held items, drawn at the body's 2-pixel unit. There are five bars, the plain pitch copper bar among them; the alloy is the tier III pick's recipe (copper and iron bars and the iron pick), not a bar.

The flare: the warning, then the flare

The vent's glow builds by alpha (the serpent's phase-warning register) and the miner steps back. A column of white fire rises to the roof and fades, embers fly, slag crusts the face (fading in), and brimstone crusts round the vent. The miner comes back and clears the crust a blow a cell.

Freeing God, a link at a time

The cage's front is cut away, leaving its frame. God hangs on two chains. The links come off one at a time from God's end, alternating sides, and each falls and lies. God drops, lands and walks out. The halo breathes brighter as each link goes. God's look is a placeholder and the owner's call.

Lives returned

In God's glare a mote of God's light (purple: magic) travels to each soul's rim, and the twelve pieces fade from pale to black as the motes arrive, alternating between the two. Then the middles and the outfits fade to the yard's ink.

4. The forge's board

The shape of the game's sheet, with its tokens copied (light: white paper, black ink; dark: #1f1f1f paper, #e0e0e0 ink, spark #f06a55). Every card is an eight-rung ladder in tierRows bands (HELL_BANDS), and its bill is its next rung's: rungs 1 and 2 brimstone only, 3 and 4 brimstone and low bars, 5 and 6 brimstone and low and high bars, 7 and 8 low and high bars and a spark. Crew rows (smiths, stokers, miners, haulers, gatherers, builders, carriers, muster) are ladders too, not counters: a member put to a face stays there for good. Only the wall gang pulls miners off their faces, so it alone has a stepper. Hell's coins have no color, so only the spark band's pips are red. Flux is the yard's ore carried down, so it keeps the yard's ore mark and blue. Bars: dross dotted, iron solid, copper banded, glass hollow, adamant latticed. Dashed means a coin is short; a gate you cannot try yet is grey and solid. Every word is a placeholder.

Open calls the mock raised

  • One pick, or one a face? The anvil's recipe consumes the old pick as the new one's core, yet the spec says a new tool is carried to every face. Either there is one pick, moved between faces and the wall gang, or every face and gang member holds one, and the recipe makes one and the rest are copies at some cost.
  • Smiths: a ladder, or "a second smith"? The spec's anvil card names "the smith's pace, a second smith, the quench tank's size". The board here makes smiths an eight-rung crew ladder, like every other crew row. Which is it?
  • Is a miner's "for good" suspended on the wall? A member put to a face stays there for good, yet the wall gang is made of miners taken off their faces. Do they go back to the same face when the wall falls, and does the face stand idle meanwhile?

What this mock cannot show

  • The game's renderer: the deep's turned palette and the dark page's map, device-pixel snapping, the wardrobe's real outfits. The pair's outfits here are stand-ins.
  • The camera moving: the glide between stops, and walking back up the run with the view's arrows. The frames are five held views.
  • Real pathing: walks are scripted on straight lines and the stair. The haulers relay so they never pass each other, but a hauler still walks through the gang's members where the floor is shared, and in option (a) the gang stands overlapped, a few pixels apart (route.js would decide both).
  • Numbers: blows, burn, batch and pace are made up for the loops; only TOOL_STROKES = 24 is config's.
  • Loop resets: each loop ends in a hold and a fade. In the game a face only deepens and a heap only grows.
  • The pile-full mark is drawn with canvas paths, as the game's warning() is, so its edge is antialiased and not on the grid. That is the game's own mark, copied.
  • Satan (call 4), the muster's arrivals down the whole road, the beats as cutscenes, and sound.
  • A phone, and the real zoom on a real screen.
