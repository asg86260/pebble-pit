The font, redrawn for the grenadiers: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The font is where the grenadiers work: each one stands at it, holds a grenade (the surface's ripples held in a ball) for three seconds, and throws it at the coil, where it bursts in rings. Each drawing is painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as they are seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), under a dome sized from the drawing's inked width (4 cells of pad, 4 of wall), on the deep's mottled floor. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that. A body square stands at the font: a ball lifts off the station into its hands, is held, is thrown up out of the dome and bursts in purple rings; the body has a keyline the game's does not, so it reads against white. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water here is a cheap stand-in), the scale bed and loose scales on the floor, the serpent overhead (the throw here aims at empty water), the board and fence, and the real grenade and ring drawing -- those are the game's own, drawn elsewhere. Nothing on a station moves: a station is painted once into an image, so the lifting ball is the body's grenade, not part of the sprite.
