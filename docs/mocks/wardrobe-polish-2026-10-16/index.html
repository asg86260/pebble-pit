Wardrobe polish (mock)

Every outfit piece checked at game size and the dangling and thin ones given a polished version, for the owner's vote. The "today" panels are the game's own drawing code (src/render/wardrobe.js, wardheads.js, wardaura.js, bundled into this page); the "polished" panels are a copy of those with only the named changes. Each live panel loops: walk right, stop, stand, walk left, stop, stand, a world pixel at a time, with the game's own clocks (the step at 4 a second, the bob at 1.2, walking still counted 0.25 s after the last step). "1x desk" is the yard at 1280x800 on a 1x screen, 0.83 device pixels a world pixel; the canvas is shown pixel for pixel. The rock behind the right of every panel is there to test a piece against a black mass.

What changes everywhere

  • Rigid at 1x. On the desktop a world pixel is 0.83 of a screen pixel, so a two-pixel unit is 1.67: today each mark rounds on its own, and as the body walks the 1 px / 2 px pattern crawls through its face, its stripes, a scarf, a ribbon. Polished: the body's corner is put on a whole screen pixel first and everything on it is drawn from there, so a piece keeps one shape while it walks. One translate in drawBody, no drawing changed.
  • Paper keyline. One unit of paper round everything worn outside the square, drawn under the body. Invisible on the bare yard; in front of the rock, a building, another body or the deep's floor it is what keeps a black cape, hat or flag from merging into the mass behind it.
  • Follow-through. Every dangle reads the walk as start / walk / stop / rest. Start: one held pose lagging before the walk poses. Stop: three held poses 0.13 s each (catching up, one unit past rest, back) before the rest pose. Still held poses on the game's clock: no tweening, no flash. It needs one more number from motionOf: how long the body has been moving or still.

Audit: every item today

Every item on a bare square, the game's own draw. Per item: standing on the sky, walking on the sky, standing in front of the rock (all 1x desk, magnified x3), walking on a phone (its device pixels, magnified x2), walking at 4x. Switch the ground at the top for the dark page and the deep.

What this mock cannot show

  • The real yard's light, smog, rain and crowd density, the deep's swell and dim, or the picker's close-up: the backdrops here are plain shapes (sky motes, the ground's hatch, a stepped black block for the rock, the deep's floor and a stone block).
  • The dark page and the deep are made here from the light draw the way the game makes them (the lightness map, the turn-over), not by the game itself.
  • The walk is a scripted loop at one speed; the game's ambles are slower, its crouch and hop are not drawn, and a carter's cart (no back item) is not shown.
  • A real phone's screen: the phone panels are its device pixels magnified, not its size in the hand (a body is nine CSS pixels tall there).
  • The keyline here is grown from the drawn pieces on a scratch canvas each frame; in the game it would be the same pass on the two bodies only.
  • The comb options in the yard use a pale purple glow the game does not draw anywhere else yet.
