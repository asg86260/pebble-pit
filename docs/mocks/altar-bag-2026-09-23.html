The altar as a punching bag: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. Each drawing is painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as they are seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), under a dome sized from the drawing's inked width (4 cells of pad, 4 of wall), on the deep's mottled floor. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that. A body square jabs the bag (from either side, or from the open side where a frame is in the way) and it swings, cell-snapped; the body has a keyline the game's does not, so it reads against a white post. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water here is a cheap stand-in), the scale bed and loose scales on the floor, the serpent overhead, the board and fence, and how brawlers really move -- in the game they swim up and punch the serpent, and nothing on a station moves: a station is painted once into an image, so a swinging bag would be new drawing code, not a sprite edit.
