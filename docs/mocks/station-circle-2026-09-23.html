The circle, redrawn around the scribes' work: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The circle's scribes draw sigils: rings on the floor, either side of the station, that hold the length of coil over them so it cannot thrash or heal. Each drawing is painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as they are seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), under a dome sized from the drawing's inked width (4 cells of pad, 4 of wall), on the deep's mottled floor. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that. A body square (keylined, as the game's is not) walks out to a slot ten cells from the station's middle, the game's SIGIL_GAP, and draws a ring there the size of the game's (7 across, 2 deep, the purple crawling round it), first one side then the other. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water here is a cheap stand-in), the scale bed the rings really lie on, the coil overhead and the thread each ring sends up to it, the board and fence, and the twenty seconds a real circle takes to draw. Nothing on a station moves in the game: a station is painted once into an image, so every option here is a sprites.js edit and nothing more.
