The well as a rack of spears: four options

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The lancers' station (keyed well in src/deep/sprites.js) redrawn as a rack of the lances they throw. Each drawing is painted the way the deep paints a station: a cell a character, in the five tones as they are seen down there (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), under a dome sized from the drawing's inked width (4 cells of pad, 4 of wall), on the deep's mottled floor. Each lance is drawn the way a thrown one is in the water: a white point, a pale shaft, a purple crest every third cell. Left is the game's own size (a cell is 6 px), right is four times that; the white square beside it is a lancer, for scale. The last pane is today's well, for comparison. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water here is a cheap stand-in), the scale bed and loose scales on the floor, the serpent overhead, the board and fence, the neighbors either side, and a lancer drawing a lance -- a station is painted once into an image, so a lance leaving the rack would be new drawing code, not a sprite edit.
