The default station: away from the crate

The owner: "lets get away from the crate. lets try something else generic." The default prop stands beside every station whose Recruit has not picked a class yet, and its footprint is the ground the build's scaffold stands round. Here is today's crate and six replacements that say "a station waiting for a class" without belonging to any class. All keep A's register: three greys lit from the upper left by A's shading rule, unchanged, and no accent and no rung-8 parts, because the default has no class. Each is drawn standing on its plinth, and again going up inside the scaffold, with the scaffold derived from that option's footprint the way drawScaffold derives it today.

What this mock cannot show: this page is drawn from plain cells, not the game. It cannot show the fighter at the post (the dotted 3x3 square marks where the body stands), the kits, the scale heaps, the motes, the pointing hand over an unchosen station or the light page. The scaffold row shows the prop as a faint ghost for reference; in the game the scaffold stands round empty ground. Colors are the abyss ramps' raw tones, not the dark page's mapped ones.
