Serpent Surface Glimpses

A little life on the drowned pit: now and then the serpent shows a bit of itself at the surface, the way the trash-compactor monster does, and goes back under. Three options, each a minute of the surface with two glimpses in it, on the 6px grid in the yard's palette: the snatch's hide (black edge, dark scales, grey brow) and the crest's two purples.

This is a mock. It is drawn from plain shapes, not by the game. It cannot show: the real abyss field (the flow, the veil, the depth-lit stars; these are a cheap imitation), the real swell's three waves, the portal standing over the pit, the camera and zoom, bodies and grain streams, or the game's frame pacing. Time is squeezed: each pane plays two glimpses a minute so you are not waiting, where the game would wait the randomized interval written under each one.

What it touches

  • Clicks: nothing. No hit area; the glimpse is drawn under the bridge and under every pointer target, and a click over it lands wherever it would have anyway.
  • Grains: nothing floats on the abyss today (a grain going in dives and is gone, leaving its notch), so there is nothing for a hump to push. A glimpse picks a stretch of surface away from any stream that is landing, so a grain's notch and the glimpse's rings never pile up.
  • Bodies: the bridge never moves; a walker crossing it is not touched. In option C the eye turns toward the nearest body on the lip, which is only looking.
  • When not: never during the snatch or a cutscene, never with the camera in the deep, never on the columns under the portal's mouth once it stands, and never two at once.
  • Rings: a grain's ripple today is a white notch on the surface's top cell, which against the white above reads as a dip. The glimpse's marks stand a cell up in pale grey instead, so a ring reads as a crest running out; the grain's notch would be left as it is.
  • Sound: none proposed (the hits-only rule).

Open question

Should the glimpses start only after the snatch, when the player knows the serpent, or as foreshadowing once the pit drowns, before the snatch? Before it, a glimpse is a promise of what is down there (and the crest's purple is the only hint of the color); after it, it is a reminder that he is still down there with the one it took.
