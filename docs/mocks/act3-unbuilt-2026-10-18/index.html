act 3: a station before it is built, and how it goes up

The owner: an unbuilt station shows nothing, just cloud floor; stations are only seen on the shack's board. Ordered from the shack, members carry a pile of materials onto its spot, each load set down and faded onto the pile; then builders take from the pile and the station's bands fade in from the bottom up, the pile shrinking as the station grows, until the pile is gone and the station stands. Here: the saw mill (take 2, through the beams), an empty spot, and the press, a station's width of floor between them. The station raised on the spot is the workshop's shed. Drawn at the yard's scale (a cell six px, a body three cells).
