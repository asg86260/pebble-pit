Station Choice Effects, round 2

Two graphical takes on D (the two specialists side by side, their effects at the foot). The status effects are named, bold and underlined -- "applies Bleed" rather than "inflict bleeding" -- and a hover on one says what it does in a line. Hover any bold word below.

A mock in plain HTML and canvas pixels styled like the board, not the game's board. It cannot show: the bills' coin marks, the pip tree as the game draws it, a phone's tap for the hover, or the fade when a branch closes. The icons are stand-ins drawn here, not glyphs from the game's editor.
