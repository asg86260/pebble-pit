The deep's bench: a general station, or a generic altar

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. The floor strips are the whole deep, 520 cells across and 130 down, at two screen pixels a cell: the stations at their DEEP_SPOTS fractions, each under a dome sized from its drawing's inked width (4 cells of pad, 4 of wall), the crusher at its spot, three pods, the portal's deep end at DEEP_PORTAL_AT with its real radius and drop, and a flat stand-in for the coil at COIL_Y. The bars under the floor are each crew's standing ground (DEEP_STAND_W either side of its spot); the bench has no crew and so no bar. The sprite cards are the deep's five tones (# white, + light grey, - dark grey, o the black water, * the abyss's purple), at the game's size and four times it. What it cannot show: the real abyss field (the water is a cheap stand-in), the serpent's body and its sway, the scale bed, bodies at work, the fences and boards, and how much of the floor the camera shows on a given window -- it opens on the portal and scrolls from there.
