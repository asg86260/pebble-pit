The deep's glyphs -- options, 2026-09-24

Every station on the deep's floor and every row on its boards is wearing a drawing borrowed from the yard. One row per drawing needed: the dashed card is what it wears now, the lettered cards are the options. Each is shown at 1x, 3x, 6x, then (at 2x) plain and in the four strokes a deep rung wears -- scale grey, crop green, ore blue, spark red -- then on every row that would borrow it, with that row's badge. The code line under each is the entry for GLYPHS in src/glyphs.js.
