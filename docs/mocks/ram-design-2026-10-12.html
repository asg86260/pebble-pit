The ram: four new designs beside today's, and D with a spinning bore

A mock drawn from plain shapes on the 6px cell grid, not the game. It cannot show the rider on the roof, the build site, the real rock eaten column by column (the face here stays put while chips fly), the drive back at a new boulder, the wind, or the real rate a tuned ram strikes at. Each scene is the same ground: the shack's wall at the left, RAM_CLEAR (3 cells) of bare ground, the machine's 11-cell body, RAM_REACH (8 cells) of daylight, then the face.

Every option keeps the 11-cell body, so ROCK_FLANK_CLEAR (14 = 3 + 11) holds without a change; what differs is height and what crosses the 8-cell gap. Smears are drawn in light grey on the frame after a fast move; nothing flashes and nothing shakes. Soot puffs use the machines' soot tones (SMOG_TINTS.mach) and fade over 3.2 s as the game's do. D1 to D3 are the pick, D, with a spinning bore for its working end: the spin is one held step a frame at 12 frames a second, and the chips curl off the cutter before they fall. D2's wheel is drawn face-on, a cheat a side view needs for the turn to read at all.
