Act 3: God, benevolent on the surface

This is a mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. It redraws the built figure with the game's own cells, greys and dark-page map: the tower throne, the robe, the crown, the glare, the halo and its rays, drawn as a light source (raw tones) on both pages. The horns, the scowl, the slit eyes and the teeth are gone. Each option below is a face on the same square. A roar is shown as a holy proclamation: the mouth opens round, the right hand rises in a blessing, the bubble is the game's. It cannot show the sim, the crowd's life, the gaze's sweep or the unmasking.

Recommendation: (c), serene

  • (a) closed-eye smile reads kindest at a glance but is a cartoon of bliss. With his eyes closed the gaze searchlight has no source, and he looks asleep at 800 by 600, where the arcs are one pixel tall.
  • (b) open kind eyes reads friendliest up close, but the round eyes and small smile are childlike. At 4 px cells it is a smiley; a giant on a tower reads as a mascot.
  • (c) serene, half-lidded keeps the pupils, so the searchlight still comes from his eyes and the glance still reads. The calm lids and quiet smile read as holy and a little remote, which is right for a disguise. It is the one the unmasking can turn: the lids lift and the smile goes, with nothing added.
  • In every option the roar opens the mouth round and lifts the right hand, so the big beat reads as a proclamation, not a snarl.
