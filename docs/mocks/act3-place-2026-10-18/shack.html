Act 3: the construction shack, and the picks together

A follow-up to index.html. The owner's votes: floor A (the billow edge), layout A, pedestal C (the cloud pillar), throne B (the cloud switchback). The crowd is being re-mocked separately, so here it is round 1's drawing, left as a stand-in. On the shack: "not a fan of any of those. make it a shack with a hammer icon."

A mock, drawn from plain cells in a canvas, not the game. It uses the game's grid (P = 6), the game's greys, and an 18 px body with a 2 px rim. The shacks are drawn in the yard's own building grammar (render/shack.js): one black mass, a lid a cell proud each side, a four-cell way in cut white, and one mark. Each take exaggerates that one mark, the hammer, the way the polished act 3 stations push their one thing. Every word is a placeholder: names are working names (like this), and the labels on the canvas are placeholders too.

1. The shack, three takes

Each take is drawn twice on floor A: left idle, right ordering a station. The ordered station's glyph (a saw blade, the yard's floating glyph) fills over the shack a cell at a time. A member carries timber (grey) out to the site, and the station rises in black bands, each fading in.

A. Sign over the door

A board hung over the door, a cell wider than the wall each side, with the hammer in ink on it. It reads plainest as a sign ("this is the place for hammers"). It is the tallest of the three, 15 cells, and the board is a second white shape beside the door.

B. Hammer cut out of the wall recommended

The yard's shack exactly, with its hill over the door swapped for a hammer cut white out of the front, as big as the wall allows. It is two cells taller than the yard's shack (13 against 11), uses the same grammar as the outhouse's moon, and the hammer reads at 1x.

C. Giant hammer on the roof

A low shack with a hammer twice its width standing on its roof: the most exaggerated. It is the only take with a moving part. While an order is in, the hammer knocks, held up a cell and then down. At 1x it reads as a hammer before it reads as a shack.

2. Beside the yard's shack, at 1x

The yard's own shack as render/shack.js draws it (8 by 11, the hill cut over the door), a body for scale, and the three takes idle, all on the yard's flat ground.

3. The picks together

One strip, 2400 px, at 1x: floor A, layout A, shack B ordering the chipper, the cloud pillar with the pair speaking (bubbles rise, motes fall to the pile), and the cloud switchback to the throne, with the tithe line walking out from God's heap and climbing it. The other stations are still round 1's placeholder boxes (their own looks are voted on elsewhere). The crowd is a stand-in. The dashed frame is roughly what the camera shows, with the counter in its corner.

What the mock cannot show

  • The game's renderer: these are plain cells. The yard shack is redrawn from render/shack.js's rules, not cut from a shot.
  • The camp's other stations. They are placeholders here, and their polished looks are a separate vote.
  • The crowd, which is being re-mocked. Round 1's file stands in.
  • The shack's board, the pointer, sound, and the camera's pan.
  • The sim: orders, walks and the tithe line are loops on a clock.
