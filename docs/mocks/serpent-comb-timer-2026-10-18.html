The comb as the feeding timer

The comb fills head first over the whole window; when the last spike lights, the bolts leave it and the meal runs as now. The window is squeezed to eight seconds here (the game's are 18 to 40).

What the mock cannot show: the real coil's bends and the head's shape, the real window lengths (a spike every one to three seconds in the game), the stun and Held pausing the fill, the haul and the gulp after the beam, and the orb phase, whose own attack lights the comb too.
