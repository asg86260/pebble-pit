Refund / buy next, split

A mock drawn from plain boxes, not the game. Today a card with a copy in the works has a REFUND ×n strip at its foot and the card's body silently queues one more -- the owner's note (2026-09-27): "its hard to tell that clicking the queued item will buy another one." Each option below splits or relabels the strip so the buy is a thing you can see. The cards are at the real shelf's size (140px slot, 141px tile, 18px strip). One model drives every plank, so pressing any of them moves all of them. What it cannot show: the real glyphs, fonts and pips, the hover tip and the tile's lift, a bill of several coins (B's buy half has room for one coin and a number -- a two-coin bill would not fit in 70px), dust grain by grain from the real pile, the queue card, or a phone, where each half is 70 x 18 against the 44px touch rule.

-- the purse fills slowly, so now and then a card is short and a press on it does nothing (greyed title, dashed tag, a shake here only to show the press was heard).

A -- two halves: refund | another

REFUND ×2 on the left, + ANOTHER on the right. The body keeps buying too (both do the same thing), so nothing a player already does breaks; the half is there to say so. The price stays on the tag above, which is already the next copy's bill.

B -- two halves, the buy half carries the price

The right half is + ▪40 -- the next copy's bill -- and goes grey when you cannot pay. The tag above stops being a price and reads the build time only. The buy and its cost are one object; the catch is the width, one coin and a number at most.

C -- a stepper: − ×2 +

Minus refunds the newest, plus queues one more, the count in the middle. The most familiar shape (a quantity field); the count reads as "how many I have ordered". Each button is 34px wide -- the smallest targets here.

D -- as A, but the body stops buying

Same strip as A. Once a copy is in the works, a press on the body does nothing (it buys the first copy only); every repeat goes through + ANOTHER. No hidden action left on the card at all, at the price of a smaller target for the repeat and one habit changed.

E -- no split: the tag says plus

The strip stays REFUND ×2 as today; the price tag gains a + while a copy is in the works, so the body is visibly "one more for this". The smallest change; the plus is small.
