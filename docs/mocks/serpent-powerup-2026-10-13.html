Serpent power-up

Mocks for DESIGN.md, "Endless serpent", after the vote on the recoil: the owner chose rears back "but with a bit of a power up animation". Each emptied bar: the serpent rears (the built pose, head thrown back, jaws wide), then gathers itself into the next, tougher level, and ends at rest with the bar full and the level ticked. Three power-ups; vote one. Nothing here is built.

This is a mock, drawn from plain shapes on a canvas, not the game. The coil is laid from the game's own numbers (length, head, neck, girth and tail taper, the sway, the crest, the jaws' hinge and gape, the cell grid), and the rear is the built one: a smear frame of 0.08 s past the pose (1.35 times as far), the pose held 0.7 s. What follows the hold is each option's own, timed below; all three end inside 2 s (built today: 1.74 s from the empty bar to the bar full).
  • As built since this morning's ask: the risen serpent's bar has no pips, only the level after it, and its belly no cage of ribs; the mock draws it that way.
  • What it cannot show: the fighters, their blows and numbers, the creatures and the meals, the orb, the statuses and the stun ring, the hide's ward and plates as the resist draws them, the fang dropping from the belly, the real bar's shards and its pixel-font level (the number here is the page's font), and the camera; the body is laid along x rather than along its arc, so a steep bend is a little thinner here than in the game.
  • The rest line (an aid for this page, not in the game): where the body would be at rest, in dark grey behind, through the rear and the power-up. Pause to hold a frame; 1/4x to read the steps.
  • No shake, no white flash, no squash tween: every frame is a held pose on the cell grid; a smear is one held frame past the pose. The purple is the deep's magic purple (the ward's ramp).

A. Flares tail to head, then snaps shut

Held in the rear, the hide turns the abyss's purple a stretch at a time from the tail up to the head, a bright front on each step. When it reaches the jaws they snap shut: one smear frame with the head thrown past its rest and the crest lit, then the head at rest. The purple drains back head to tail in three steps as the bar refills in six held notches, the level ticking as the jaws shut.

Timing
smear 0.08, rear held 0.7 (the flare, seven steps of 0.1), shut 0.08, then the bar 0.6 (six notches) with the drain over its first 0.36. 1.46 s.
Reads as
A charge running up the body and spent in a bite; the snap is the beat the new level starts on.
Cost
A purple body for most of a second, where the magic ward is also purple: on a magic-resist level the two read alike.

B. Draws the deep in

Held in the rear, the coil winds tighter in three held steps, and motes leave the water from all round it, purple and grey, each crossing in four held steps with a dim smear behind and landing on the body with a spark. Each mote landed is a notch in the bar, so the bar fills as the deep pours in; the level ticks with the first. The last one in, the coil lets go in one smear frame past the rest line and settles.

Timing
smear 0.08, rear held 0.7 and down in the built three steps (0.36); motes from 0.15 s to 1.8 s, 40 of them, 0.4 s a crossing; the let-go at 1.8 (0.08 smear, then rest). 1.94 s, the longest.
Reads as
The serpent feeding on the deep itself to come back stronger; the bar's refill has a cause on screen.
Cost
The busiest of the three: the motes cross the stations' water and the fighters under the coil.

C. Ratchets the hide

From the rear the head comes down in three held steps, and with each step the hide stands up thicker and its plates (the physical resist's bands) rise a cell further off its back, a purple seam lit down the spine and along the plates' edges on the click, the spine holding a dim purple through the rest of the step. Each click is a third of the bar, the level ticking on the first. Then the hide settles back to its girth in one held impact frame, the crest a cell taller and the seam full purple, and rest.

Timing
smear 0.08, rear held 0.7, three clicks of 0.2 (the built back steps and refill folded together, 0.6), the settle 0.08 lit and 0.08 dim. 1.54 s.
Reads as
Armoring up: three beats you can count, a level tougher; the most mechanical of the three, closest to the physical resist's plates.
Cost
Three cells thicker at the third click, so the belly comes down near the fighters' heads for a moment.
