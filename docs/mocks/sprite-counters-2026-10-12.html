Sprite Crowd Counters

Three counters instead of two crowds: carters, workers and forklifts, each drawn with the body the yard already draws for it, in place of today's square pips under a brim. Each strip is the ground line with the house's and the garage's footprints, the garage on the left a GARAGE_CLEAR (144px) off the house as in the game, and the counters at the post's line, ten cells under the ground. Shown for a small yard (3 carters, 2 workers, 1 forklift) and a big yard (20, 30, 8), each at 1x (one world pixel a screen pixel, so a cell is 6px; the game's own cell is 5px, a touch smaller) and magnified 2x.

A mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. The sprites are the game's own drawing code (drawBody, drawCart, drawCartBox, drawLiftBox from src/render/crew.js) copied in verbatim and rasterized here; the small ones are those same pixels halved, two-by-two. It cannot show live counts (a body walking over, the half-filled pip of one on its way, the count easing up), hover, the house's lit windows, or anything else in the yard around them.

The sprites

Full size as the yard draws them, and halved for a counter. A carter is its body with the cart hitched behind; a forklift is the truck with its mast, forks and antenna, nobody on it.
