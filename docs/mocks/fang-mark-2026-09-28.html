The fang's mark on the build button

A mock drawn from plain cells, not the game. Each mark is nine pixels square and is laid on a copy of the floating build button the way the game does it -- only the ink cells are drawn, as a mask in the text's color, so a mark's "paper" cells are simply empty. Shown at the button's real size and at four times, over black water, and on the counter card's line. It cannot show the real deep behind the button, the button's fade, or the camera moving it. Light or dark follows your system's setting.

For reference: the fang itself in the water (FANG_SPRITE, 3 cells, a cell 6 px)
