Into the Deep

The owner's ask: a short scene the first time the player enters the abyss. The camera finds the captured sqhusband in the serpent's cage of ribs. Then the sqwife makes her first attack on the serpent, and the player takes over. Each pane opens on the end of the ripple out of the portal, because that is how the player arrives today, and then plays one option. The purple bar under each pane is the stretch that hold-space skips. The tick marks where the player takes over.

This is a mock drawn from plain shapes, not the game. The coil, the cage, the two bodies, the bang and heart marks and the punch follow the shots of deep, deep-wound, serpent-head and snatch-leap. The mock cannot show the real hide shading, the fish, the currents, the stations or the roster, the objective card, the real camera's zoom steps (the game snaps zoom to whole device pixels), or the dark-mode deep (the game inverts it). Lines in [brackets] mark where the owner's words would go. The boxes of grey bars on the canvas stand in for them.

What every option shares

  • It plays once, the first time the player arrives in the deep, as a new beat in BEATS (beats.js) owning the camera. It goes into beatsDone, and a reload mid-scene replays it from S.beat.camera, like every camera beat.
  • It starts when the ripple (view.js, VIEW_GLIDE_S 1.3 s) has swept the window, not before: the ripple is the way down and is not part of the scene.
  • Hold space (SKIP_HOLD_MS) skips. Like the existing cutscenes, a skip lets go of the camera but never the moment: she still throws her first blow, and the coil still does what it did.
  • The camera lets go without going home, the cutscene.js rule: it eases out to the deep's own frame over CUT_OUT_S, and the serpent's bar returns as it does.
  • No letterbox, no flash, no shake. The deep keeps running underneath. Only the camera and her first blow are staged.

What clashes with the deep as built

  • She is already fighting. She leaps in as the snatch ends and is the first station's fighter from then on. The deep runs whether anyone watches it, so by the player's first visit she may have been punching for an hour, with cracks open. "Her first attack" is either the first one the player sees, or her fighting waits at the portal until the first visit. That is a sim change, and the owner's call.
  • The first visit can be long after the snatch. The way down opens only once the wizards' portal is bought (canGoDown), so the scene keys on the first arrival, not on the snatch.
  • The ripple plays on every way down (VIEW_GLIDE_S). The scene must wait until it has swept the window, or the first ripple could open on the belly instead of the portal.
  • cutscene.js only frames the yard. stepShot sets a ground line (camLockY) and lets go to zoom 1, and the deep takes back its own zoom every frame in stepView. The kill's killFrame is the only deep camera move today.
  • stage.js does not reach the deep. It skips bodies off the yard. At a first visit the deep is usually just her, but delvers or bought fighters would need a deep version, or should be left in the shot.
  • Any click skips a cutscene (skipCutscene), and in the deep a click is a strike, so the player's first click would be eaten by the skip. Hold-space alone may be the better skip down here.
  • The serpent keeps living: a fish can swim in for a meal mid-scene, it heals, and the bar and the objective card sit over the shot. Whether these pause, hide or carry on is open.
