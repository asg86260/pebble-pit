Each creature its own click

Every creature in the deep, drawn from its own cells in the game, with two candidate reactions to a click (A and B). Each loops: a second of its idle swim, the click (the small grey cross), the reaction, and home again. Click a panel to play its reaction now.

This is a mock, drawn from plain shapes on a canvas, not the game. The zone creatures' drawings are copied cell for cell from FAUNA_SPRITES (src/config/fauna.js) with the game's tone rules (render/fauna.js: per-cell grey nudge, purple ramp, blinking lights, the midnight band two rungs dim); the eight stream creatures are the draw functions of render/fish.js copied as they are. The reactions, poses and timings are new and are the proposal.
  • Today: the nine zone creatures swimming (FAUNA, one each) are clickable: a click within the bird's reach startles one, shakes loose the bird's handful (5 scales that sink for the gatherers) and it bolts for the nearer end of the deep in held poses, away 20 to 40 s. The eight stream creatures (render/fish.js: the near stream over the coil, the serpent's meals and now most of what is on screen, and the far stream) have no hit area at all: those are the ones not clickable today. Three zone kinds are switched off (n: 0) and sit at the end.
  • Per reaction a line says whether today's click (the scale drop, and the bolt) stays alongside it. The "scales" switch shows or hides the drop in every panel.
  • What it cannot show: the real deep around them (the coil, the stations, the portal, the camera and its zoom, the phone's scale), the zone tint as it will be (another change is reworking the band dithering; the water here is a flat stand-in dither of each zone's two tones), the far stream's parallax and darker tones (the stream creatures are drawn here at near-stream tones and size), the scales landing on the real bed and the gatherers fetching them, how many creatures there will be (the count is being cut separately), sound, and the hit area (a click anywhere on a panel plays it).
  • Left out: gulper (the gulper eel) has a drawing in the code but no row in FAUNA, so it never swims; it is not drawn here. The meal's swim into the mouth and the rift-life crossings are pictures of the same eight stream creatures and are not separate kinds.
