Three calls the design left open

The locked list

The design says unearned notices are not named, only counted (14 of 52) — the rule the books and the counter already follow: nothing in this game names a thing you have not met. But plenty of players want a list to work through, and a bare count may read as coy rather than as restraint. One predicate either way.

The walk

Whether a body comes over from the houses and stands at the board for a beat when a notice lands — the way the crew already dance when a rock comes down — or whether the bobbing tick over the station is the whole of it. The walk is the most in-register version of “the yard noticed”; it is also an errand, a route and a state, for a thing that is already legible without one.

Where a body’s own record is shown

records.js has kept a name, an age, and what each body has mined, quarried, farmed and stored since the crew were made people, and nothing in the game has ever displayed any of it. The record sheet is one candidate; the crew list off the house board is the other. Named here so it does not get solved twice.
