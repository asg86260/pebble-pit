A controller plays the whole game, the story has a card of its own, and the yard looks and reads more alike from page to page.

New

  • Controller support: the d-pad hops between the places a click lands, A does what a click does, and the Steam Deck's back buttons and trackpads come mapped.
  • The story card: a card at the top left names the story's next step, and the next barrier and the next place are bought from it, coin by coin.
  • No more cores: each barrier that falls opens the next place instead, the farm, the quarry and then the tower.
  • Calls for help: your sqwife yells from under the rock while it is mined, in a few different ways.
  • Cutscenes: the yard holds still and goes letterboxed, and everything but the scene fades back.
  • The index: every thing you have met, with a few words on each, on the pause sheet or the i key.
  • Language page: pick a language by its own name from settings, with the new translations behind it.
  • Feedback page: send your words, and your save if you tick it, from the pause sheet.
  • Crew pips and hats: every counter is a row of pips, any miner or farmer can wear a blaster or grower hat, and five bought unlock the drill and the tiller.
  • Crew view: hold the right button to ring every worker; add the left to pick one up.
  • A shorter pit: it fills and tears open about three times sooner, and once it has drowned, things surface at the abyss and cross the sky behind the rift.
  • A new ram: a tracked crawler that bores into the rock with a turning cutter wheel.
  • A visual and menus pass: one grey and one purple ramp, one type scale, pixel numbers on every count, and a controls page listing the mouse and keys.
  • Saves: each save shows its playtime and has its own delete.
  • Times board removed: the board of rescue times is gone.

Fixed

  • Two haulers no longer go for the same pebble, and haulers pick up what is near them before walking to the hole.
  • Saving the sqwife before the pit drowned no longer leaves it solid.
  • A left click no longer gives a free handful of pebbles while the belt runs.
  • Quarriers, balloons and rockhands come back where they should after a reload or when the serpent falls.
  • Language and card layouts no longer overlap in Japanese, Korean, German, French, Portuguese and Russian.

The full list of fixes is in the changelog.
