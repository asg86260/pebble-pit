PEBBLE PIT

A rock. A hole. A few people between them.

GAME EMBED / HERO GIFThe opening dig: the first body swings at the rock, carries two grains the length of the yard and tips them into the hole. 1280 x 800, click to start.
RUN GAME

Click once to start: sound and keys wake on the first click.

A boulder falls on your sqwife, and you start digging.

Every pixel you knock off the rock is a grain of dust, and every grain has to be carried to the pit. Spend it on a crew who mine, haul and build where you can see them: nobody teleports, and a station with nobody standing in it does nothing. Black and white, on a six-pixel grid, with no music -- only the sound of the yard.

What it is

  • Nothing teleports. Every worker walks to every job and carries what it moves.
  • A crew of people. Each worker has a name, an age and a record of what they have shifted.
  • Pick them up. Drop a worker across the yard; they walk back to work.
  • They take breaks. Idle hands smoke, sing and talk -- never while there is work.
  • Six grays, one grid. A light page and a dark one, and a reduced-motion switch.

Things to know

  • Nothing happens while it is closed. The yard runs while you watch it.
  • No fail state. Nothing is taken away for walking off.
  • Your save lives in this browser. It autosaves every second; save a copy on the pause sheet takes it with you.
