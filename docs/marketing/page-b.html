About this game

A boulder falls on your sqwife, and you start digging.

Every pixel you knock off the rock is a grain of dust, and every grain has to be carried to the pit. Spend it on a crew who mine, haul and build where you can see them.

Nothing teleports

Every worker walks to every job and carries what it moves. A station with nobody standing in it does nothing.

GIFA hauler sweeping dust off the ground and tipping it into the pit

A crew of people

Each worker has a name, an age and a record of what they have shifted. Pick one up and drop them across the yard; they walk back to work. With nothing to do, they smoke, sing and talk.

GIFA break: two workers turned to each other, dots going back and forth

Black and white

Six grays on a six-pixel grid, a light page and a dark one, and no music -- only the sound of the yard.

Played at your pace

No fail state and no offline progress: the yard runs while you watch it. It autosaves every second, and a save can be copied out and loaded back.
