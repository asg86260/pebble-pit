MOCK, not the game: act 3's timber-scaffold stair, built from the floor up

Plain shapes, not the game: outlined boxes are the stations, black 3x3 squares are workers (a bar or an upright post over a head is a carried piece), the white crowned square is God on his throne, pale tiers are cloud. The stair is built bay by bay (a bay is 6 rows): posts rise from the floor and fade into place, then beams and X-braces, then a working deck and the way up (take-specific); the builder only ever stands on the floor or on a deck already built. The treads are laid last, from the top down, while the builder descends, so the flight appears with the crew coming back to the floor. The camera pans right along the camp, stops going right at the column and then scrolls straight up; the minimap marks the turn. The words in the corner are a mock HUD, not game copy. It cannot show the real sprites, the real worker speed (the mock plays 2x), the night palette, or the no-overlap footprints; feet on ramps and ladders are interpolated.
