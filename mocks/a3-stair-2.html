MOCK, not the game: act 3's switchback stair, round 2: three scaffolds

Plain shapes: the stair is option C of round 1 (switchbacks up a cloud column), now held up by scaffolding, with railings and landings. Each bought increment is one flight plus the scaffold under it, carried up piece by piece (a plank bar or an upright post over a worker's head) and faded in. Outlined boxes are the stations (forestry, saw mill, workshop, chipper, paper mill, press), black 3x3 squares are workers (a bar over the head is a carried plank), the white square with a crown is God on his throne, pale tiered shapes are cloud, and the black-and-grey stepped ribbon is the stair. The corner box is a minimap (the camera is the small frame) and the top-left words are a mock HUD tally, not game copy. Each panel loops: a price tag appears on the next empty stair section, the cursor buys it, the tally ticks down, two workers carry planks up from the press and the section fades in. The camera follows the build along its own path. It cannot show the real sprites, the real worker speed or the real camp walk, and a worker's feet on a slope are interpolated, not planted on each tread.
